= Repository-wide claim graph
:revdate: 2026-09-11

== Authority

`data/claim_graph.csv` is the repository-wide claim authority.
`claim_id` is unique across every product. `product_id` selects a publication or
analysis surface without creating a second scientific truth.

The graph records, per claim:

* empirical status;
* identification status;
* causal status;
* policy usability;
* optional linkage to an active `data/scientific_state.csv` component;
* evidence scope and evidence artifacts;
* allowed and forbidden interpretations;
* supersession relationships; and
* whether the claim is active.

`data/claim_evidence.csv` is the evidence-edge table. Multiple evidence rows may
support one claim. Evidence rows whose `claim_id` is absent from the claim graph
are invalid.

== Product projections

`scripts/generate_claim_registries.py` deterministically projects the authority
into:

* `paper1/data/claim_registry.csv`; and
* `research/vat_claim_registry.csv`.

The Paper 1 projection preserves the existing seven-column public contract:
`claim_id,topic,status,evidence_scope,allowed_claim,forbidden_claim,source`.
The Issue #44 migration requires all P1-C01 through P1-C17 rows to reproduce
without semantic loss.

== Semantic ordering

Claim-level identification may be more conservative than its linked component,
but it may not be stronger. The enforced order is:

----
NOT_IDENTIFIED < SENSITIVITY_ONLY < PARTIALLY_IDENTIFIED < READY
----

`RECOVERY_ONLY` is outside that promotion chain. A recovery-only claim must have
recovery evidence and cannot satisfy an active reproduced claim.

Likewise a linked claim may not claim stronger causal identification than its
component, and `POLICY_USABLE` is forbidden when the linked component has
`policy_usable=false`.

== CI invariants

`scripts/validate_claim_graph.py` and `scripts/test_claim_graph.py` make CI fail
for at least the following classes:

* duplicate repository-wide claim IDs;
* orphan evidence edges;
* active claims without active evidence;
* active claims supported only by recovery evidence;
* declared evidence artifacts without matching evidence edges;
* missing source/evidence paths;
* claim/component identification, causal or policy contradictions;
* stale product-specific generated registries; and
* missing VAT coverage for the compliance-productivity and policy-integration
  components.

The claim graph does not upgrade scientific status. In particular, the VAT
institutional GDP channel remains model-contingent sensitivity, and the VAT
policy matrix remains a partial integration rather than a policy forecast or
joint welfare optimum.
