== Independence requirement

The reviewer must qualify as `INDEPENDENT_REVIEWER` or stronger under
`docs/scientific_reviews.adoc`.  The reviewer should state any prior authorship,
implementation role, financial interest, or other conflict that could weaken
independence.  An internal adversarial review cannot satisfy either track below.

== Track 1: ECONOMIC_IDENTIFICATION

Assess whether the data, assumptions, and reproduced calculations support the
manuscript's exact identification claims.  At minimum, challenge all of the
following.

. *Population semantics*: Is the 2024 F71561 mother population correctly treated
  as a published 2020-Census-based frame benchmark rather than an observed 2024
  person population?  Are tax units, persons, households, filers, and return
  records kept distinct throughout?
. *Stage-1 mismatch frontier*: Does the manuscript keep the total untracked
  mismatch budget `C_U` unresolved?  Do `R(C_U)` and `C_U^*(A)` remain robustness
  functions rather than an unconditional filing-rate estimate or confidence
  interval?
. *Cross-system tax-event semantics*: Are the pseudo modeled-annual-tax-positive
  event and the NTA positive self-assessed return balance after credits and
  withholding kept distinct?  Is any rate discrepancy incorrectly described as
  measurement error between observations of one event?
. *Rank transport*: Do `Delta`, `epsilon`, and `eta` remain explicit relaxation
  budgets/compatibility parameters rather than estimated rank maps, confidence
  regions, or causal bounds?  Are v3/v4 symmetric-gap and v5 one-sided results
  interpreted as answers to different model questions?
. *Marginal incidence*: Does any text imply that the pseudo-filer construction is
  an observed or point-identified filer MTR distribution?  Are sensitivity-only,
  recovery-only, and `NOT_READY` results excluded from headline identification
  claims?
. *Missing same-person linkage*: Does the absence of public same-person
  salary/return/payment/refund/source-withholding linkage invalidate any active
  claim, or is the limitation stated narrowly enough?
. *External validity and policy use*: Does the manuscript avoid converting its
  identification results into an optimal tax rate, macro effect, or policy-usable
  replacement-financing conclusion?
. *Claim registry consistency*: For every headline sentence, is there an active
  claim whose status and evidence support language at least that strong?

For each challenge, classify the finding as `BLOCKER`, `IMPORTANT`, or `PASS` and
identify the exact file/section.  A `BLOCKER` is any issue that makes a headline
identification claim unsupported or materially changes the stated contribution.

== Track 2: MANUSCRIPT_MAJOR_REVISION

The repository records that this track must check the manuscript against a prior
Major Revision critique.  The *full original critique is not currently versioned
in this repository*.  Therefore this gate must remain `PENDING` until the
independent reviewer receives an authenticated copy of that critique (or it is
otherwise archived with provenance).  This packet does not reconstruct missing
review text and must not be treated as an exhaustive substitute.

Once the original critique is available, the reviewer should produce a
point-by-point disposition (`RESOLVED`, `PARTIALLY_RESOLVED`, `UNRESOLVED`, or
`SUPERSEDED_BY_NARROWER_CONTRIBUTION`).  In addition, assess the revision themes
that are already explicit in the current repository:

. Is the paper consistently framed as an identification/robustness study using
  public aggregate data, rather than as a point-identified optimal-policy or
  macro-distribution paper?
. Do the title, abstract, introduction, tables, and conclusion use the same
  narrower contribution language?
. Are observed/reproduced results visually and verbally separated from
  sensitivity-only and recovery-only material?
. Are provenance, data availability, reproduction instructions, and source
  boundaries sufficient for an independent reader to audit every headline
  result?
. Are limitations and next-data requirements specific enough that missing
  identification is not hidden behind model output?
. Does the related-literature section support the claimed novelty without
  overstating what this paper identifies?
. Is any remaining issue severe enough that journal targeting or formatting
  should wait for another substantive revision?

== Required reviewer return

Return separate dispositions for the two tracks.  Each completed review record
must contain:

* review track and independence class;
* exact reviewed commit;
* reviewer conflict/independence statement;
* numbered findings with severity and file/section locators;
* explicit resolution criterion for every `BLOCKER` or `IMPORTANT` finding;
* final disposition: `PASS`, `PASS_WITH_IMPORTANT_REVISIONS`, or `BLOCKED`.

If one independent reviewer covers both tracks, the repository must still record
separate review IDs because the promotion gates are track-specific.  Completed
review artifacts belong under `reviews/`, have `evidence_eligible=false`, and may
never be used as claim evidence.

== Promotion rule

Do not change either pending Paper 1 gate to `SATISFIED` merely because this
packet exists or because CI is green.  A gate can be satisfied only after the
corresponding independent review is complete, its findings are resolved as
required, and `data/scientific_reviews.csv` links the completed review to the
exact reviewed commit.  Until then `paper1` remains `WORKING_MANUSCRIPT`.
