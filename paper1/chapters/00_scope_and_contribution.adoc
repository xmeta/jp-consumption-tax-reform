== Scope and contribution

This paper studies a narrower question than the companion consumption-tax
policy project: what can be learned about Japanese personal income-tax
marginal incidence from public aggregate data when filer microdata are not
available for a fully reproducible analysis?

The paper does not estimate an optimal VAT rate, an optimal macro policy mix,
or a structural GDP effect.  Those questions require additional behavioral and
macro identification and are deliberately outside this submission.

=== Contributions

First, we treat population mismatch as an identification problem rather than
silently mapping household aggregates into filer microdata.

Second, we distinguish observed moments, partial identified sets, reference
selections, sensitivity constructions, recovered prior-run evidence, and
quantities that remain not identified.

Third, we derive a reproducible stage-1 filing-participation robustness
frontier.  The result leaves the unresolved number of frame-incompatible
final-return filers explicit rather than imputing it.

Fourth, after pre-specifying the model before inspecting endpoints, we report a
replacement transport-relaxation frontier that quantifies how much discrepancy
is required to jointly accommodate the pseudo-filer sensitivity system and
NTA primary-income-type stage-2 composition.  Its MTR envelopes are explicitly
model-contingent and are not presented as identified sets.

Fifth, every substantive statement is tied to a machine-readable claim
registry, making overstatement detectable during revision.
