== Stage-1 filing-participation frontier

The first selection step asks how many F71561-compatible candidate persons can
be guaranteed to overlap the NTA final-return population.

We deliberately avoid estimating a filing probability by pseudo-filer fitting.
Instead, we construct a worst-case overlap bound and expose the remaining
population mismatch as an explicit nuisance count.

=== Conservative population bridge

Let stem:[F={stage1-final-return-filers}] denote NTA FY2024 final-return persons,
stem:[P={stage1-candidate-upper}] the total 2020 Census population used as a conservative
candidate-person upper bound, and stem:[M_L={stage1-mother-lower}] a conservative
lower bound on persons represented by the F71561 mother population.

The Fréchet lower overlap before additional contamination is

[stem]
++++
B_0 = \max\{0,F-(P-M_L)\}={stage1-overlap-lower}.
++++

The construction uses the exact four-municipality Okunoto exclusion and a
deliberately loose person denominator.  The denominator therefore weakens,
rather than strengthens, the inferred filing-participation lower bound.


=== Adversarial tracked contamination

To avoid optimistic alignment assumptions, we allocate several observable
population-timing channels entirely against the overlap:

* all 2020--2024 births: stem:[3{,}836{,}677];
* all 2020--2024 gross international inflows: stem:[2{,}643{,}204];
* every 2024 death as one potential quasi-final return:
  stem:[1{,}605{,}378].

Their sum is

[stem]
++++
D={stage1-tracked-upper}.
++++

This is intentionally conservative.  The categories can overlap, many persons
are not filers, and the full-year 2020 counts include events before the Census
reference date.

After allocating stem:[D], the residual overlap at zero additional mismatch is

[stem]
++++
B_0-D={stage1-residual-cnr0},
++++

which corresponds to {stage1-cnr0-pct}% of the candidate-person upper bound.

=== Unresolved frame-incompatible filers

Let stem:[C_{NR}] denote additional final-return filers incompatible with the
resident/Census bridge, principally unresolved non-resident cases.

The identified robustness frontier is

[stem]
++++
L(C_{NR})=
\frac{\max\{0,{stage1-residual-cnr0}-C_{NR}\}}
{{stage1-candidate-upper}}.
++++

For an admissibility threshold stem:[A], rejection occurs whenever

[stem]
++++
C_{NR} <
\max\{0,{stage1-residual-cnr0}-{stage1-candidate-upper}A\}.
++++

For the recovered V6 `score_power=2.0` threshold ({stage1-score2-threshold-pct}%), the
critical value is

[stem]
++++
C^*_{NR}={stage1-critical-cnr}.
++++

Thus that prior is rejected for every
stem:[C_{NR}<{stage1-critical-cnr}].  The `score_power=0.5` and
`score_power=1.0` thresholds are not rejected even at stem:[C_{NR}=0].
