== Populations and public data

The analysis connects public statistics that describe different populations.
The central empirical difficulty is therefore not merely sampling error; it is
the absence of a public micro-level crosswalk between those populations.

=== F71561 household population

F71561 reports household and equivalized-income aggregates by income decile and
household type.  It contains income components and income-tax liability
moments, but it is not a filer-level tax-return file.

The 2024 survey frame is built from the 2020 Population Census, rearranged to
2024 municipality boundaries.  The mother-population construction excludes the
four Okunoto municipalities and removes student single-person households from
the eligible single-person population.

=== NTA Table 2-1 Final-return processing-row benchmark

For the stage-1 public benchmark we use the FY2024 NTA annual-statistics
Table 2-1 row labelled `Final return`, {stage1-final-return-filers} persons.
Table 2-1 reports taxation status as of June 30 for persons who filed or whose
cases were processed by March 31.  The row is therefore treated as an exact
processing-row aggregate, not as the count of all persons whose return forms
were submitted by the March deadline.  The separate NTA filing-status press
release reports 23,389 thousand submitted-return persons at displayed
thousand-person precision.  We also do not use the broader 23,362,184
Table 2-1 filed-or-processed total.

The NTA Table 2-1 processing-row benchmark and the F71561/Census-based population are not
assumed identical.  Their mismatch is carried as an explicit contamination
parameter in the identification analysis.

=== Ordered NTA income classes and within-survey rank diagnostics

For the rank-based sensitivity analyses we separately use NTA Annual Statistics
Table 2-2(1), which provides 25 ordered total-net-income classes for the
table's administrative processing population.  Its denominator is the broader
23,362,184-person Table 2-2(1) population, not the stage-1 Table 2-1 Final-return processing-row
benchmark used above.  The 125 positive-self-assessed-balance cells by income class and
primary income type cross-check exactly to the independently extracted NTA
Sample Survey Table 2 cells.

Here positive self-assessed balance means the NTA concept `申告納税額のある者`.
The NTA sample-survey definition subtracts tax credits and tax already paid
through source withholding from calculated tax before determining this balance.
It is therefore not equivalent to positive total annual income-tax liability.
The pseudo-filer event used later is a different object: modeled annual income
tax greater than zero.

Two additional 2024 NSFCW tables are retained as diagnostics for the strength
of rank-bridge assumptions.  Table 7-141-1 reports main annual-income-type
composition across OECD-new equivalized-disposable-income deciles, and Table
7-171 reports equivalized-disposable-income quantiles by main annual-income
type.  These tables show substantial within-survey composition differences
across rank but do not provide an observed mapping to NTA primary-income
categories or tax records.  They therefore motivate sensitivity analysis
without entering the v3 LP as hard cross-system constraints.

=== Unit discipline

Income-tax liability is an individual tax concept, whereas F71561 is reported
at household/equivalized-income cells.  Household reconstruction therefore
cannot by itself reveal the true filer-level statutory marginal-rate
distribution.
