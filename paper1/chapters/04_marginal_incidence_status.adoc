== Marginal-incidence identification status

The stage-1 result does not by itself identify the filer-level statutory
marginal-tax-rate distribution.

=== Reproducible pseudo-filer sensitivity construction

The repository now contains an independently reproducible pseudo-filer
sensitivity core under
`research/income_tax_pseudofiler/`.

The core is generated from repository-tracked official-source-derived inputs:
F71561 household-type/decile aggregates, the F71911 household-size and age
bridge, F71531 tax and social-insurance moments, and versioned 2026 National Tax
Agency statutory parameters.  The raw official source files, source locators,
and SHA-256 values are retained in the repository.

For each decile and sensitivity scenario, the construction allocates aggregate
income components to pseudo tax units, applies the statutory income
transformations and tax brackets, and chooses a nuisance income scale to match
the observed aggregate income-tax liability moment or, at statutory
discontinuities, the nearest achievable moment.

The nuisance scale is a calibration device.  It is not a behavioral,
structural, or causal parameter.

The construction evaluates thirteen explicit scenarios over ten income deciles, including NTA Table 17 salary-class and F71551 pension-age external sensitivities.
The generated outputs and executable tests are regenerated in CI.  Its
reproducibility status is therefore
`SENSITIVITY_ONLY_REPRODUCED`.

This is a reproducibility statement, not an identification statement.

The construction still does not observe:

* filer-level income;
* filer status for the F71561 household cells;
* within-cell joint income and deduction distributions;
* actual household tax-unit partitioning;
* filer-level age assignment;
* the true joint distribution of taxable income and statutory marginal rates.

Accordingly, the generated pseudo-filer MTRs may parameterize ETI or stress-test
scenarios, but they cannot supply the headline identified incidence result and
cannot be treated as point-identified inputs to optimal-policy conclusions.

The historical V6 file `18_income_tax_pseudofiler_mtr.py` remains a separate
`RECOVERY_GAP`.  The status above refers to the new independently
reproducible sensitivity core, not to byte-identical restoration of the lost
historical implementation.

=== Calibration and statutory rounding

The aggregate moment calibration uses a continuous quick-table convention,
matching the historical sensitivity-construction convention.  The repository
then recomputes ordinary national income tax under the statutory rule that
taxable income is rounded down to the nearest JPY 1,000.

These two quantities are reported separately.

The 2026 basic deduction also changes discontinuously at statutory total-income
thresholds.  Some extreme sensitivity scenarios therefore cannot hit the
aggregate liability moment exactly with one nuisance scale.  Such cases are
reported as nearest-achievable sensitivity diagnostics; the tax-law
discontinuity is not smoothed away to improve fit.

=== Pre-specified replacement transport-relaxation frontier

The historical V6 restricted-LP constraint chain remains unreproduced.  We
therefore do not reconstruct its endpoints by reverse engineering recovered
numbers.

Separately, the repository pre-specified a new transport-relaxation LP before
computing its endpoints.  The model uses all thirteen committed pseudo-filer
scenarios and the five exact NTA primary-income-type stage-2 rates.  It imposes
the published NTA primary-type composition while allowing a common absolute
discrepancy stem:[\varepsilon] between each decile's pseudo-filer
positive-tax share and its transported NTA stage-2 rate.

Two decile-weight schemes were fixed before endpoint computation: equal decile
weights and normalized F71561 leaf-household counts.  The minimum feasible
common relaxation and the conditional overall MTR envelopes are generated
directly from the research outputs:

The required minimum common discrepancy is therefore large under both
pre-specified weight schemes: approximately 24.19 percentage points under
equal-decile weights and 22.20 percentage points under F71561 leaf-count
weights.  Exact equality at stem:[\varepsilon=0] is infeasible in this model.

This gap is not a standard error or an estimate of measurement error.  It
quantifies incompatibility between two aggregate constructions *under the
stated transport model*: pseudo-filer positive-tax shares are constructed from
household aggregates, whereas the NTA stage-2 rates are observed among
final-return income earners in five primary income categories.  The model also
uses the NTA category *composition* rather than asserting equality between the
NTA Table 2-2 population total and the separate stage-1 benchmark population.

The MTR ranges in the table are conditional envelopes at
stem:[\varepsilon^*].  They are reproducible consequences of the
pre-specified transport-relaxation model, but they are not:

* the historical V6 restricted-LP endpoints;
* statistically sharp identified sets;
* confidence intervals;
* causal bounds;
* point-identified filer MTR distributions.

The historical P1-C06 restricted-LP claim consequently remains
`DOCUMENTED_PRIOR_RUN_NOT_REPRODUCED`.  The new replacement result is tracked
separately as a model-contingent sensitivity claim.

=== Static liability versus behavioral incidence

Observed F71561 income-tax liability moments can support static,
first-order liability calculations under explicitly stated accounting
assumptions.  This is the meaning of `READY_STATIC_ONLY`.

Static liability incidence does not identify:

* filer selection under a policy change;
* taxable-income elasticities;
* general-equilibrium earnings responses;
* dynamic tax revenue;
* the joint welfare effect of a VAT-income-tax reform.

Those quantities remain `NOT_READY`.
