== Limitations and data improvements

The principal limitation is not numerical optimization. It is the mismatch
between public household aggregates and the filer-level population needed for
marginal income-tax incidence.

=== Untracked frame mismatch

The 2024 F71561 mother population is estimated from the 2020 Census frame
rearranged to 2024 municipality boundaries. It is therefore not a directly
observed 2024 set of persons that can be intersected mechanically with the NTA
final-return population.

We leave all additional overlap loss beyond the deliberately tracked allowance
as stem:[C_U]. This quantity can include:

* non-resident final-return filers outside the resident/Census bridge;
* remaining 2020-to-2024 population timing mismatch;
* survey eligibility and household-frame differences;
* other unmeasured population-definition mismatches.

The reproduced result is therefore a robustness function in stem:[C_U], not an
identified point value or unconditional lower bound.

Public NTA tables examined for this study do not provide a compatible
resident/non-resident cross-tab of final-return persons. The NTA also reports
920,649 statutory-report information records concerning payments to
non-residents supplied to foreign tax authorities in FY2024, but that is a
record count rather than a deduplicated final-return-person bound and is not
used to set stem:[C_U].

=== Data that would materially tighten identification

The highest-value additional data would be:

. a directly comparable 2024 person-frame count or micro-level crosswalk
  between the survey mother population and tax-return population;
. a resident/non-resident count of FY2024 final-return persons on a
  conceptually compatible basis;
. anonymized filer microdata or sufficiently detailed tabulations linking
  taxable-income components, deductions, filing status, and tax liability;
. a direct cross-tab of income rank (or a bridgeable taxable-income band) by
  NTA primary income category and positive-liability/final-return status, so
  stage-2 rates need not be transported into F71561 deciles through an
  unobserved category mixture;
. public cross-tabs connecting household income deciles to tax-unit
  characteristics without synthetic assignment;
. repeated-year equivalents permitting validation of stability and transport.

The P1-C13 replacement transport model requires more than 22 percentage points
of common relaxation under both pre-specified decile-weight schemes.  That
result does not identify the source of the discrepancy, but it raises the
value of a direct income-rank-by-primary-category tax cross-tab: such a table
would remove one of the strongest transport assumptions currently needed.

=== Scope of policy conclusions

The reproduced results in this paper do not establish an optimal consumption
tax rate, an optimal income-tax schedule, or a structural macroeconomic effect.

A later policy-optimization paper may condition on transparent behavioral
ranges and macro models, but such results must be labelled model-contingent
unless those additional parameters are independently identified.
