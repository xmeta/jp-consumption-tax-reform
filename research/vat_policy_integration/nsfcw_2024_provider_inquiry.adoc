= 2024 NSFCW DS51–DS52 provider inquiry package
:revdate: 2026-09-13

== Status

This is a provider-inquiry and minimum-analysis specification for Issue #94.
It is not provider confirmation, access approval, or evidence that DS51 and
DS52 may be joined.

The target datasets are:

* `M4AR8-DS51` — income/assets aggregation system;
* `M4AR8-DS52` — detailed two-month-average household accounts.

Until the provider confirms the same-household join and approved data are
actually analyzed, objective-rank VAT incidence, Gini, FGT2 and real-
disposable-income effects remain `NOT_IDENTIFIED`.

== Official inquiry route

The repository source `EMICRO-ONSITE-USE-GUIDANCE` records that questionnaire-
information use is handled principally through e-Micro and that questions can
be submitted through the Microdata Utilization consultation form using the
subject `調査票情報の利用に関するお問い合わせ`.

This inquiry concerns data interpretation and permitted cross-system use, not
reservation of a physical onsite facility.
== Facts already established from official secondary metadata

The provider metadata expose `SetaiFugo` and `Ichiren` in both systems.
`SetaiID` is documented as empty/not used for the 2024 aggregation.
Those facts do *not* establish a complete or authorized cross-system key.

DS51 contains the objective-rank-side inputs, including `I_Bdy031`
(OECD-new annual disposable income), household size and population weights.
DS52 contains detailed household expenditure and separate population weights,
including `Bdy003` consumption expenditure, `Bdy004` food, `Bdy015` alcohol,
`Bdy016` dining out and `Bdy233` newspaper expenditure.

The complete variable-level authority is
`data/derived/estat_objective_rank_restricted_variable_map_2024.csv`.
The route-level authority is
`data/derived/estat_objective_rank_restricted_route_audit_2024.csv`.

== Questions that require provider confirmation

. May records in `M4AR8-DS51` and `M4AR8-DS52` for the *same 2024 household*
  be linked inside an approved onsite project?
. If yes, what is the complete provider-approved join key or composite?
  In particular, are `SetaiFugo` and `Ichiren` sufficient, or are geography,
  survey-unit, month, sequence, or other fields also required?
. Is that key technically stable and unique for this cross-system join, and is
  the join institutionally permitted under the approved use application?
. What is the cardinality between the two systems for an approved household
  join (one-to-one, one-to-many, or another documented structure), especially
  given that DS52 represents a two-month-average household-accounts system?
. Which weight and population definition should be used after joining the two
  systems, and are any adjustment or duplicate-handling rules required?
. If cross-system joining is not supported, can the provider produce or accept
  a 2024 custom tabulation that crosses the official OECD-new equivalized-
  disposable-income rank with the required household expenditure measures?

A positive answer to the first question is insufficient unless the exact key,
cardinality, weight treatment, and institutional permission are also clear.

== Minimum onsite analysis if the join is approved

Population:: 2024 NSFCW household units represented in the provider-approved
DS51–DS52 joint population. Do not infer unmatched households or silently
change the population definition.

Objective rank:: Construct the OECD-new equivalized-disposable-income rank from
DS51 `I_Bdy031` and the official NSFCW equivalization inputs/rule. Do not use
annual-income deciles as a substitute and do not invent an equivalization rule.

Expenditure side:: Use DS52 detailed household expenditure. At minimum retain
`Bdy003`, `Bdy004`, `Bdy015`, `Bdy016`, and `Bdy233`, while preserving the
existing warning that survey categories are not transaction-level legal VAT
bases.
Weights:: Use only the provider-approved joint-population weight treatment.
Do not choose between the DS51 and DS52 weight fields by convenience.

Primary disclosure-safe outputs:: Weighted household counts by objective-rank
bin and weighted expenditure totals/means for the retained expenditure fields,
plus enough diagnostics to verify join cardinality, unmatched-record handling,
and weight conservation without exporting restricted identifiers.

Reproducibility boundary:: Version the analysis code and specification in this
repository. Only provider-approved, disclosure-reviewed outputs may leave the
onsite environment. Raw questionnaire microdata and join identifiers must not
be checked into the repository.

== Fallback if the join is unavailable

Ask whether a 2024 custom tabulation can be supplied with household as the
statistical unit and the official OECD-new equivalized-disposable-income rank
crossed with the required expenditure measures. The minimum requested output is
household counts/weights plus expenditure totals or means by objective-rank bin.

A custom table is not treated as available until the provider confirms that the
2024 survey can be tabulated to this specification and states the applicable
access, fee and disclosure conditions.

If neither the onsite join nor a 2024 custom table is supported, retain the
current `NOT_IDENTIFIED` state and stop this route. Do not resume broad public-
aggregate searching without a source that satisfies the existing stop rule.

== Draft inquiry text
件名（フォーム選択）:: `調査票情報の利用に関するお問い合わせ`

問い合わせタイトル:: 令和6年全国家計構造調査 M4AR8-DS51 / DS52 の同一世帯接続可否について

----
令和6年（2024年）全国家計構造調査のオンサイト利用を検討しています。
二次メタデータに掲載されている M4AR8-DS51
（所得・資産側）と M4AR8-DS52（詳細な家計収支側）について、
同一世帯のレコードをオンサイト環境内で接続して分析できるか確認したく、
以下をご教示ください。

1. DS51 と DS52 の同一世帯レコードの接続は、技術的かつ制度上可能でしょうか。
2. 可能な場合、利用すべき正式な接続キー（複合キーを含む）をご教示ください。
   両データに見られる SetaiFugo と Ichiren だけで十分か、地域・調査単位等の
   追加項目が必要かも確認したいです。
3. 接続時のレコード対応関係（1対1、1対多等）、重複・未接続レコードの扱い、
   及び接続後に使用すべき集計用乗率をご教示ください。
4. SetaiID は2024年集計では使用しない旨が二次メタデータにありますが、
   今回のDS51–DS52接続にも使用しないという理解でよいでしょうか。
5. 上記接続が利用できない場合、OECD新基準の等価可処分所得階級・十分位と
   世帯の消費支出等を交差させた2024年のオーダーメード集計を依頼可能でしょうか。
----
