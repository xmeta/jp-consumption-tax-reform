= VAT policy scenario integration matrix
:revdate: 2026-09-10

== Purpose

This adapter satisfies the integration step of Issue #26 without inventing
unidentified macro effects. It maps the existing VAT institutional
compliance/allocation stress module into all eight requested policy scenarios
and reports GDP, growth, distribution, poverty, inflation, fiscal and debt
outcome statuses in the same rows.

It is deliberately an *integration layer*, not a replacement macro model.

== Numeric versus unresolved outputs

The adapter numerically carries:

* the model-contingent institutional GDP level component
  stem:[m(\rho c_{VAT}+a_{alloc})];
* its annualized transition-growth contribution; and
* a static FY2024 central-government VAT cash-receipt replacement reference.
The stress range remains *not an empirical bound*. Current evidence does not
identify national stem:[c_{VAT}], stem:[\rho], or stem:[a_{alloc}].

The fiscal reference is parsed from the Ministry of Finance FY2024 treasury
revenue table.  Consumption-tax cash receipts are 25,021,206,715 thousand yen,
or 25.021206715 trillion yen.  Total tax receipts are 74.187865765 trillion yen,
so consumption tax represents about 33.73% of the recorded central tax receipts.
For the 0% and full-abolition scenarios this amount is carried as a *static gross
receipt-removal/replacement reference*.  For the JGB scenario the same amount is
shown as a one-for-one gross JGB financing reference.  Income-tax, asset-tax and
mixed-financing scenarios carry it only as a replacement target.

This is not a behavioral tax-revenue forecast or a complete fiscal-balance
effect.  It excludes changes in tax bases, timing, imports, local consumption
tax, other tax receipts, expenditure, interest, macro feedback and general-
government consolidation.  The 5% VAT scenario is deliberately left unquantified
because the 10%/8% rate mix and taxable bases cannot be recovered by simple
linear scaling of aggregate receipts.

The matrix also contains explicit fields for overall real GDP, annual real
growth, growth-decline penalty, income Gini, wealth Gini, intergenerational gap,
FGT2, real disposable income by decile, inflation, fiscal balance, debt/GDP and
interest-rate/JGB-market effects.

Until the corresponding demand, household-incidence and financing modules are
integrated, their numeric cells remain empty and adjacent status fields identify
the missing link. This is preferable to assigning zero: zero would be an
economic claim, whereas an empty value plus a status is a scientific-state
statement.

== Outputs

* +data/derived/vat_fiscal_receipt_reference.csv+: observed FY2024 central
  tax-receipt metrics.
* +data/derived/vat_policy_fiscal_replacement_reference.csv+: static eight-
  scenario receipt-removal/financing references.
* +data/derived/vat_policy_scenario_matrix.csv+: 8 scenarios x 300
  institutional stress points = 2,400 rows.
* +data/derived/vat_policy_scenario_summary.csv+: one row per policy scenario
  with the institutional range and joint outcome-status vector.

The matrix is operational end-to-end, but it is not yet a policy forecast or a
joint welfare optimization. The next completion step is to replace explicit
unmodeled statuses with validated demand/distribution/financing outputs as those
modules become reproducible in this repository.
