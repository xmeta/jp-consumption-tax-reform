= Firm-level VAT compliance-to-output identification bridge
:revdate: 2026-09-11

== Purpose

This design answers a narrower question than the existing stress grid: what
firm-level evidence would actually reduce uncertainty in `c_VAT`, `rho`, or
`a_alloc`?  It does not convert the existing METI/JCCI/SMEA burden and invoice
incidence evidence into a GDP effect.

The current national parameters remain `NOT_IDENTIFIED`.  The design matrix is
`firm_level_identification_design.csv`.

== Estimands

Let `i` index firms in a stated target population, `w_i` be population weights,
`R_i` be recurring VAT-specific real resources used for compliance, and `Y_0`
be baseline output on the same population/accounting basis.

=== Real-resource cost: `c_VAT`

[stem]
++++
c_{VAT}=\frac{\sum_i w_i R_i}{Y_0}.
++++

`R_i` must measure real inputs: internal staff time valued at an opportunity
cost plus recurring purchased accounting/adviser/software resources on a
consistent basis.  Tax remittances are transfers, not compliance resource cost.
A response-time distribution without population weights or a clear period does
not identify national `c_VAT`.

=== Productive redeployment: `rho`

For the existing policy model, `rho` is the fraction of released VAT-compliance
resources that becomes productive input during the specified transition:

[stem]
++++
\rho=\frac{\sum_i w_i P_i^{released}}{\sum_i w_i R_i^{released}},
\qquad 0\leq\rho\leq1.
++++

It requires observing actual resource release and subsequent reassignment (or a
credible causal proxy).  Cross-sectional correlation between burden and sales
does not identify `rho`.

=== Allocative effect: `a_alloc`

`a_alloc` is the output-equivalent gain from removing VAT-induced allocation
distortions *excluding* the direct `c_VAT × rho` resource-redeployment channel.
Empirical threshold, registration, transaction-network, entry/exit, or
investment designs can identify local responses.  Mapping them to a national
abolition `a_alloc` requires explicit population weights, treatment transport,
and general-equilibrium assumptions; local bunching or transaction incidence is
not itself national `a_alloc`.

== No double counting

A firm outcome response can contain both released administrative resources and
allocation changes.  A reduced-form sales/value-added coefficient must not be
added to `c_VAT × rho` unless the design decomposes the direct resource channel
from the residual allocation channel.  Otherwise the reduced form is reported
as a combined local effect only.

== Candidate Japanese linkage routes

=== Preferred existing-data route for resource cost

The first route tested was a secure linkage between the existing METI
2019--2021 tax-procedure burden microdata and the METI Basic Survey of Japanese
Business Structure and Activities (BSBSA).  BSBSA explicitly collects
corporation number, employees, sales, costs, assets and investment, and METI has
a formal questionnaire-microdata secondary-use process.

Issue #82 resolves the *standard e-Micro route*.  Official e-Micro catalog
queries captured on 2026-09-11 return zero records for the exact keyword
`中小企業税制に関するアンケート調査` in both the media/remote and onsite
catalogs.  As a positive control, the same two catalog surfaces return 32
records for `企業活動基本調査`.  The public 2019--2021 reports also do not
establish a corporation number or other stable cross-survey firm identifier,
and no official evidence currently establishes permission to link those
commissioned-survey microdata to BSBSA.

The route is therefore `STOP_STANDARD_EMICRO_RETROSPECTIVE_LINKAGE`.  This is a
stop decision for the standard catalog route, not a claim that archived
microdata or an internal identifier never existed.  It may be reopened only if
the provider explicitly confirms all three prerequisites: archived burden
microdata availability, a stable firm key, and cross-survey linkage permission.
The machine-readable decision is
`firm_level_linkage_access_audit.csv`.

=== Prospective burden/redeployment panel

If the historic link is unavailable, the minimum useful new survey is not
another burden-incidence cross-section.  It must record VAT-specific internal
hours and recurring external resources, stable firm identifiers, population
weights, pre/post firm outcomes, and a defensible source of burden variation.
Only then can it estimate national `c_VAT` and a local `rho`; without an actual
or quasi-exogenous burden reduction it would merely reproduce the current
identification gap.

=== NTA Theme 5(ii): corporation tax + consumption tax

The National Tax College sixth-call materials explicitly allow combined Theme 2
corporation-tax and Theme 4 consumption-tax microdata.  The official synthetic
schemas expose hashed corporation identifiers.  Theme 2 contains principal
industry, capital, sales and corporation-tax income/tax fields; Theme 4 contains
VAT taxable standard, VAT amount, input-tax credits, payment/refund fields,
general/simplified regime status and taxable-sales measures.

This is the strongest currently advertised tax-microdata route for local
`a_alloc` work around thresholds, regimes and reforms.  It does not contain
VAT-compliance hours or released-resource reassignment, so it does not identify
`c_VAT` or `rho`.  The sixth-call application window closed on 2026-04-30; the
repository therefore keeps a future-call design ready rather than pretending
that access is currently open.

=== Invoice registry + BSBSA

For a taxable corporation with a corporation number, the NTA registration rule
constructs the qualified-invoice registration number as `T` plus the 13-digit
corporation number.  BSBSA collects corporation number.  This gives a
technically deterministic corporate linkage between public invoice registration
history and firm outcomes *if* the approved BSBSA microdata environment permits
a linkable privacy-preserving corporation key.

Registration is endogenous and the invoice system was a broad national reform.
An event study or DiD therefore needs a defensible comparison group/timing,
parallel trends, and controls/design for endogenous registration.  Even a valid
local effect would shrink `a_alloc` uncertainty only locally; registration
status is not a measure of `c_VAT` or `rho`.

=== Cross-ministry NTA + METI linkage

Combining NTA Theme 5(ii) with BSBSA/Business Register outcomes would be useful,
but the current NTA joint-research call does not advertise such cross-ministry
linkage.  It remains `INSTITUTIONAL_LINKAGE_NOT_AVAILABLE_NOW`.  The design must
not bypass agency access controls or infer a confidential join from names and
addresses.

== Quantified uncertainty reduction

The matrix quantifies the immediate identification gain as the number of the
three model dimensions whose admissible set would shrink if the design succeeds.
This is intentionally a count, not a fabricated numerical confidence width.

* historic METI burden × BSBSA secure link: `1/3` (`c_VAT` on the overlap
  population; national transport still required);
* prospective burden/redeployment panel: `2/3` (`c_VAT` and local `rho`), with
  only `c_VAT` potentially national under a valid probability/reweighting frame;
* NTA Theme 5(ii): `1/3` (local `a_alloc`-relevant response);
* invoice registry × BSBSA: `1/3` (local `a_alloc`-relevant response);
* NTA × external firm statistics cross-ministry extension: `1/3` under a future
  approved linkage.

No current candidate identifies all three national parameters.  Consequently
`c_VAT`, `rho`, and national `a_alloc` remain stress/sensitivity inputs until an
executed design produces evidence that satisfies the stated selection,
representativeness, and causal conditions.

== Decision

The execution order is:

. treat the standard e-Micro historic METI burden-microdata linkage route as
  stopped under Issue #82;
. specify the prospective burden/redeployment panel as the next executable
  `c_VAT` / `rho` research design;
. independently retain NTA Theme 5(ii) as the best existing route to local
  allocation-response evidence when a compatible future call opens;
. test invoice-registry × BSBSA deterministic linkage only inside an approved
  microdata environment;
. do not spend current effort on unadvertised cross-ministry NTA/METI linkage.

This closes the design problem.  It does not close the empirical identification
problem.
