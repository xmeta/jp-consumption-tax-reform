= 2023 invoice-system causal-evidence and natural-experiment design
:revdate: 2026-09-13

== Status

This is the execution specification for Issue #111.  It separates direct
post-2023 descriptive evidence, historical Japanese VAT quasi-experiments, and
new causal designs.  It does not promote any current repository claim to
`CAUSAL_IDENTIFIED`.

A bounded literature search through 2026-09-13 did not locate a public study
that estimates firm-outcome effects of Japan's October-2023 qualified-invoice
introduction with a defensible DiD, RDD, event-study or natural-experiment
counterfactual.  This is a search-frontier statement, not proof of universal
nonexistence.  The machine-readable audit is
`invoice_2023_causal_literature_audit.csv`.

== What can already be learned

Direct post-introduction evidence exists.  The SME Agency/TSR and JCCI surveys,
and Fujimaki (2025), document reported registration pressure, administrative
burden and transaction adjustment.  These sources are useful for outcome and
heterogeneity selection but do not supply an untreated counterfactual.

Historical Japanese VAT studies provide stronger mechanism evidence:

* Suzuki and Kawakubo (RIETI 21-E-090) exploit historical VAT reforms and a
  manufacturing panel; their local structural/bunching estimates attribute the
  observed small-enterprise output response mainly to compliance costs rather
  than tax rates.
* Ichikawa, Arudchelvan and Onji (2019) use the 2014 VAT-rate increase as a
  quasi-experiment around the 10-million-yen exemption threshold; the relative
  bunching comparison supports a compliance-burden mechanism but predates the
  invoice-based trading-partner incentive.
* Hosono, Hotei and Miyakawa (RIETI 19-E-041) show threshold responses vary with
  productivity and compliance-cost conditions.

These studies justify taking real behavioural distortions seriously.  They do
not identify the causal effect of the 2023 invoice system and are not used as a
numerical 2023 treatment coefficient.

== Primary design for the 2023 introduction

The preferred direct design is predetermined supplier exposure x post-2023.
For firm `i`, define `E_i` only from supplier relationships and VAT/exemption
status observed before October 2023.  Post-treatment registration choices or
post-treatment supplier composition must never define `E_i`.

A dynamic specification is:

[stem]
++++
Y_{it}=\alpha_i+\lambda_t+\sum_{k\ne -1}\beta_k
(E_i\times 1[t-T=k])+X_{it}'\gamma+\varepsilon_{it}.
++++

The coefficients before the event are specification diagnostics, not effects.
The design is admissible only if pre-trends, common support and the frozen
exposure definition survive audit.  Industry-by-time controls and placebo dates
are required because the national reform coincides with sector-specific shocks.

This design is not publicly executable now: the repository has no lawful panel
that jointly contains pre-2023 supplier-level VAT status/exposure and repeated
firm outcomes.  Aggregate survey shares are not substituted for that missing
joint data.

== Prospective 2026 natural experiments

The FY2026 reform creates additional policy variation that can identify invoice
burden mechanisms without relying on endogenous registration timing.

=== Legal-form relief differential

The current 20-percent special measure ends with tax periods containing dates
through 2026-09-30.  Under the current reform, individual proprietors can use a
30-percent special measure for 2027 and 2028, while corporations do not receive
that extension.  A legal-form x ex-ante-eligibility x post design can therefore
estimate a marginal seller-side burden effect if comparable pre-trends and
common support are demonstrated.

The treatment must be aligned to each tax period.  A raw individual-versus-
corporation comparison is invalid because legal form is selected and associated
with size, industry, financing and growth.  Matching/reweighting, dynamic
pre-trends, eligibility interactions and simplified-taxation transitions are
minimum diagnostics.

=== Non-qualified-supplier input-credit step

The current rule is not the previously scheduled 80%-to-50% step.  The FY2026
reform changes the first step from 80% to 70% from October 2026, with later
50%, 30% and 0% phases.  It also lowers the per-supplier purchase cap from
1 billion yen to 100 million yen for affected tax periods.

A buyer-side exposure design can interact a *pre-step* share of purchases from
non-qualified suppliers with the October-2026 policy change.  The 70% share
shock and the cap shock must be coded separately; either restrict to transactions
safely below the cap or estimate distinct cap-exposure terms.  Using 80%-to-50%
in an analysis would be a stale-rule error.

Both 2026 designs are pre-registerable now but cannot yet deliver mature post-
period estimates as of 2026-09-13.

=== Frozen pre-period registry capture

Before the October-2026 policy step, Issue #111 freezes a privacy-safe public-
registry baseline using the NTA download interface.  The source manifest covers
11 full-snapshot ZIPs dated 2026-08-31 and 40 daily delta ZIPs from 2026-07-15
through 2026-09-10.  It stores official file IDs, download URLs, byte sizes and
SHA-256 hashes, but no invoice registration numbers.  Identifier-level ZIPs are
processed only in a local temporary directory and are not committed to the
public repository.

The accompanying observation snapshot separates three objects: (1) surviving
full-snapshot registration/cancellation/expiration history effective by the
2026-08-31 cutoff, (2) latest-row stock and already-announced future-effective
events, and (3) daily publication-change rows from the 40-business-day delta
window.  Full-history counts exclude records already removed by process `99`;
delta publication rows are not interpreted as effective event dates.  This
prevents survivorship and future-date leakage from being silently treated as
causal pre-trends.

The capture is `DESCRIPTIVE_REGISTRY_PRETREND_NOT_CAUSAL`.  It can later support
pre-trend, manipulation and implementation diagnostics for a 2026 design, but
without a treated/control exposure and firm outcomes it identifies no economic
effect.

== Designs that are not causal by themselves

`INVOICE_REGISTRY_X_BSBSA` remains useful as a deterministic corporate linkage
backbone if the provider permits a privacy-preserving corporation key.  Invoice
registration timing itself is endogenous.  A naive registered-versus-
unregistered or early-versus-late event study is therefore diagnostic only.

A difference-in-discontinuities design around the 10-million-yen VAT exemption
threshold is also potentially useful, but only with the legally relevant
base-period sales variable and explicit treatment of bunching/manipulation,
voluntary invoice registration and concurrent relief measures.  Conventional
RDD continuity cannot simply be assumed at a manipulated threshold.

The ranked design matrix is `invoice_2023_natural_experiment_design.csv`.

== Minimum data request

The preferred 2023 design requires, in one approved environment:

* stable firm identifiers and repeated outcomes from at least 2021 through 2025
  (longer post-period coverage preferred);
* supplier-customer identifiers and transaction/purchase values observed before
  October 2023;
* pre-treatment VAT/exemption status of suppliers or another provider-approved
  pre-treatment variable that identifies invoice exposure without using later
  registration choices;
* sales/output, employment, investment and exit/survival outcomes where
  available;
* industry, size, region and legal form for common-support and trend controls;
* invoice registration history for mechanism and compliance diagnostics, not as
  the sole causal treatment definition.

The 2026 designs additionally require tax-period dates, 20%/30% special-measure
eligibility/use, base-period taxable sales, simplified-taxation status, and/or
supplier-level non-qualified purchase exposure and cap exposure.  A data provider
must confirm linkage and disclosure rules; probabilistic name/address matching
of confidential records is prohibited.

== Promotion rule

A local estimate can shrink `a_alloc` uncertainty only for the population and
margin identified by its design.  It does not identify national VAT-abolition
GDP effects, `c_VAT`, or `rho`.  Promotion to a causal repository claim requires
all design-specific diagnostics plus the independent identification review gate.

Until then the repository state remains unchanged: current JCCI/SMEA/Fujimaki
invoice evidence is descriptive; historical VAT quasi-experiments are mechanism
and design evidence; national `a_alloc` remains `NOT_IDENTIFIED`.
