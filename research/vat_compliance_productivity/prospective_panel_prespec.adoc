= Prospective VAT burden-redeployment firm panel pre-specification
:revdate: 2026-09-11

== Scope

This is a design artifact for Issue #84, not evidence that any model parameter is identified.
The machine-readable definitions are `prospective_panel_estimand_map.csv` and
`prospective_panel_variable_dictionary.csv`.  No effect size, sample size, or
national parameter value is inferred here.

== Identification targets

The panel separates three objects that must not be collapsed.

* `national_c_vat` is a population-weighted real-resource-cost estimand.
* `local_rho_internal` is the fraction of measured *internal staff* VAT hours
  released by a valid intervention that is productively reassigned in the
  treated/complier population.
* model `rho` covers every real resource represented in model `c_VAT`.  If
  adviser/software/provider resources are included in `c_VAT`, internal staff
  redeployment alone cannot identify model `rho`.

`a_alloc` is outside this panel's identification scope.  Firm outcomes may be
reported as a local reduced form, but they do not become national `a_alloc`.

== Sampling and target population

A national `c_VAT` result requires an approved sampling frame that supplies
known, non-zero inclusion probabilities for the stated target population.
Industry, size, legal form and VAT-regime strata are fixed before sampling.
Oversampling is allowed only when the inclusion probabilities are retained and
undone by the analysis weights.

If no approved probability frame is available, a recruited/convenience pilot
may still test measurement and treatment mechanics, but every estimate is
labelled pilot/frame-specific.  It must not be called national `c_VAT`.

Corporations and sole proprietors are either both covered by the stated frame or
reported as separate target populations.  Nonresponse and attrition adjustments
are pre-specified from frame/baseline variables.  Complete-case and weighted
results are reported separately.  Loss to follow-up is never coded as business
exit without independent evidence.

== VAT-specific real-resource measurement

At W0 and W1 firms complete a fixed four-week VAT-task diary, supported where
possible by activity/accounting-system logs.  Time is split into:

* qualified-invoice tasks;
* VAT calculation/return tasks; and
* VAT-specific incremental recordkeeping.

Ordinary bookkeeping that would remain without VAT is recorded separately and
is not charged to `c_VAT`.  Internal hours are valued with a role-specific
opportunity-cost rule fixed before analysis.  VAT remittances are transfers and
are never counted as compliance resources.

Adviser and software payments are private expenditure measures, not automatically
social real-resource cost.  They enter model-compatible `c_VAT` only if provider
VAT-specific labour/capital resources are directly measured or linked through a
pre-specified validated conversion.  Without that bridge, the study reports an
internal-resource partial estimand and keeps national model `c_VAT`
`NOT_IDENTIFIED`.

== Treatment and variation for `rho`

The preferred design is a randomized VAT-task-specific burden-reduction
intervention or encouragement.  It must not bundle sales, finance, inventory,
general bookkeeping, or other non-VAT productivity functionality.

A quasi-random policy/timing design is admissible only under a separate frozen
design specifying assignment, exclusion/continuity assumptions, manipulation
checks, and comparison population.  A descriptive cross-section is never a
`rho` design.

The first stage is change in measured VAT-specific internal hours.  Released
hours and their destinations are then measured directly.  Productive and
nonproductive/unused reassignment must reconcile with released hours within a
pilot-fixed tolerance.  Assignment without observed resource release and
reassignment does not identify `local_rho_internal`.

== Waves and outcome windows

The minimum sequence is frozen as follows.

* W0: baseline 12-month accounting outcomes plus a four-week VAT-task diary.
* W1: immediately pre-intervention confirmation and treatment assignment.
* W2: one to three months after treatment start; repeat task diary/log and
  resource-reassignment module.
* W3: nine to twelve months after treatment start; accounting outcomes on a
  definitionally consistent 12-month basis where available.

Calendar dates may move with implementation.  Relative windows, variable
definitions, primary outcomes, and treatment rules cannot be changed in response
to observed effects.

== Outcomes and channel separation

Pre-specified outcomes are sales, value added (or an explicitly named proxy),
employment, investment and exit/survival.  W3 reduced-form effects are local
diagnostics and may combine direct compliance-resource and allocation channels.
They are not added to `c_VAT * rho` unless a design separately decomposes the
direct resource channel from residual allocation effects.

== Weighting and national transport

For the national path, the analysis weight is built from inverse inclusion
probability, pre-specified nonresponse/attrition adjustment, and any documented
calibration to frame margins.  Missing population coverage cannot be repaired
by silently post-stratifying a convenience sample.

Treatment effects apply initially to the eligible/complier population.  Moving
from a VAT-task intervention to nationwide institutional abolition requires a
separate transport argument covering treatment eligibility, treatment-vs-
abolition differences, nonrespondents, uncovered legal forms, and external
service providers.  That transport is not assumed by this pre-specification.

== Privacy and linkage

Analytic data use a pseudonymous `panel_id`.  Direct identifiers are stored
separately under the approved data-protection protocol.  Corporation number or
public invoice history may be linked only when lawful and within the documented
consent/provider approval.  Restricted official microdata are linked only
inside the provider-approved environment.  Probabilistic name/address matching
of confidential records is prohibited.

== Pilot and futility gates

Do not field a costly full panel merely because a pilot can be recruited.
Stop, redesign, or restrict the estimand when any of these structural gates
fails:

. VAT-specific work cannot be separated reliably from ordinary bookkeeping.
. The national path lacks known inclusion probabilities; the study may continue
  only as a pilot/local design.
. Purchased adviser/software payments cannot be mapped to provider real
  resources; model-compatible national `c_VAT` remains unidentified.
. The intervention bundles material non-VAT productivity functions.
. The pilot 95% confidence interval for the treatment first stage on VAT
  internal hours does not establish a reduction below zero.
. Released hours and productive/nonproductive destinations cannot be reconciled
  under the frozen measurement rule.
. Attrition or linkage loss cannot be characterized well enough to define the
  analysis population and weights.

Passing these gates only authorizes a larger study.  It does not identify a
national parameter before the required data are actually observed.
