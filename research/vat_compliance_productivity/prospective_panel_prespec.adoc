= Prospective VAT burden-redeployment firm panel pre-specification
:revdate: 2026-09-11

== Scope

This pre-specification is the Issue #84 fallback after Issue #82 stopped the
standard historic e-Micro linkage route.  It defines the minimum panel that
could reduce uncertainty in `c_VAT` and local redeployment behavior without
turning a burden survey into a GDP estimate.

The authoritative machine-readable contracts are
`prospective_panel_estimand_map.csv` and
`prospective_panel_variable_dictionary.csv`.  Before data exist, national
`c_VAT`, national `rho`, and `a_alloc` remain `NOT_IDENTIFIED`.

== Population and sampling

The target for a national `c_VAT` claim is active private businesses in Japan
that incur or could incur VAT-administration resources during the measurement
period.  A national claim requires a probability frame with known inclusion
probabilities and coverage appropriate to that target.

The Statistics Bureau Business Register is the preferred frame in principle:
it is continuously updated and designed to retain information on all
establishments and enterprises.  Its official provision page, however, limits
provision to approved administrative bodies, local governments and specified
independent administrative institutions for statistical purposes.  This
repository therefore does not assume direct academic access.

Two execution tiers are pre-specified:

. *National-inference tier*: an eligible public/statistical partner draws the
  sample from the Business Register (or another frame demonstrated to have
  known inclusion probabilities and target-population coverage).  Base weights
  are inverse selection probabilities, then response/attrition adjustments and
  pre-specified calibration are applied.
. *Pilot tier*: a recruited or commercial frame may be used to test task
  measurement, identifiers and the randomized burden-reduction first stage.
  Unknown inclusion probabilities automatically disable national `c_VAT`.

The 2024 Economic Census is a useful public calibration diagnostic, but its
private-establishment scope excludes some units, explicitly including
individually operated establishments with no employees.  Published Census
margins alone therefore cannot repair an uncovered all-business sampling frame.

Pre-specified stratification variables are industry, employment-size class,
legal form and region, with deliberate oversampling of small/threshold- or
invoice-exposed firms allowed only when selection probabilities remain known.
Nonresponse and panel attrition are adjusted from frame/baseline covariates;
remaining non-ignorable selection is reported as sensitivity rather than
assumed away.

== Measurement period and waves

A short one-off burden questionnaire is insufficient.  The minimum design has:

* `W0`: enrollment, frame variables, fiscal/VAT status, baseline accounting
  outcomes and initiation of an event/time diary;
* a pre-treatment task-measurement window covering recurring VAT work;
* `W1`: the first complete post-activation VAT reporting/compliance cycle;
* `W2`: an aligned mid-horizon outcome/redeployment measurement;
* `W3`: at least 12 months after activation so recurring and annual/episodic VAT
  tasks can be reconciled over a complete annual cycle.

The primary annual `c_VAT` estimand is not produced until a complete 12-month
cycle is observed or an independently validated annualization mapping exists.
The design does not extrapolate four-week diary minutes to an annual total by
simple multiplication.

== VAT-specific resource measurement

Every time event is assigned a pre-specified task type and specificity class:
`DISAPPEARS_WITH_VAT`, `RETAINED_GENERAL_ACCOUNTING`, or `MIXED_UNCERTAIN`.
Only the first class enters the primary recurring VAT numerator.  Mixed/shared
bookkeeping enters sensitivity analysis; tax remittances are transfers and are
excluded.

Internal time is valued using role-specific opportunity-cost measures.
Recurring external adviser/accountant/software spending is reported separately
as a private-cost proxy.  A model-compatible real-resource `c_VAT` additionally
requires provider-side VAT-specific real resource inputs or a separately
identified price-to-resource mapping.  One-time setup, migration and training
are transition resources, not recurring `c_VAT`.

Value added, not sales, is the preferred denominator for aggregation because
summing sales across firms double counts intermediate transactions.

== Causal design for redeployment

A descriptive panel cannot identify `rho`.  The prospective design therefore
includes an embedded randomized offer of a standardized VAT-specific workflow
automation/assistance package *only if* its provider-side resource use can be
metered.  Assignment is the primary treatment; adoption is secondary and may be
instrumented by assignment under the pre-specified exclusion, monotonicity and
no-interference assumptions.

The first causal estimand is deliberately narrower than model `rho`:
treatment-induced productive reassignment of internal staff resources divided
by treatment-induced reduction in internal VAT resources.  A model-compatible
local `rho` is reported only if external/provider resources and transition
inputs are also measured so that net total resource release is defined.
Neither local estimand is automatically transported to national full VAT
abolition.

Sales, value added, employment and investment are secondary reduced-form
outcomes.  They are not added to `c_VAT * rho` as an extra allocation dividend
unless the direct resource channel is separately decomposed.

== Linkage and privacy

The analysis dataset uses a study-generated pseudonymous `panel_firm_id`.
Corporation number may be used only inside an approved trusted linkage boundary
to create a non-reversible analysis token; raw corporation number is not stored
in the analysis dataset.  Public invoice-registration history may be linked for
corporations only when legally and institutionally approved.  No name/address
probabilistic matching is used to bypass access controls.

== Pilot and futility gates

The full panel is not fielded merely because the questionnaire can be deployed.
The following gates are pre-specified:

* `FRAME_GATE`: if inclusion probabilities/coverage needed for the national
  target cannot be established, disable the national `c_VAT` claim; continue
  only an explicitly local/pilot design.
* `SPECIFICITY_GATE`: if pilot respondents cannot reliably separate VAT-specific
  tasks from retained general accounting, stop the `c_VAT` measurement design
  and revise the instrument before scale-up.
* `IDENTIFIER_GATE`: if a lawful stable linkage token cannot be maintained,
  drop linked administrative outcomes rather than constructing a probabilistic
  confidential join.
* `ANNUALIZATION_GATE`: if a complete VAT compliance cycle/12-month task record
  cannot be obtained, do not report annual `c_VAT` from diary multiplication.
* `PROVIDER_RESOURCE_GATE`: if intervention/provider resource inputs are not
  measured, `RHO_MODEL_LOCAL` remains unidentified even if internal hours fall.
* `FIRST_STAGE_GATE`: if the randomized offer does not reduce the pre-specified
  VAT-resource first-stage with a 95% confidence interval entirely in the
  intended direction, stop `rho` scale-up and do not interpret outcome noise as
  redeployment.
* `ATTRITION_GATE`: if treatment-dependent missingness cannot be handled by the
  pre-specified weighting/bounding analysis, stop the causal `rho` claim.

No final full-panel sample size is invented before the pilot.  The pilot supplies
variance, compliance, clustering and attrition inputs for a separately frozen
power calculation.  Any eventual minimum detectable effect must be selected for
policy relevance before outcome inspection.

== Claim boundary

Successful execution can estimate a frame-specific VAT private-cost share and,
with stronger provider-resource measurement, move toward a model-compatible
`c_VAT`.  A valid randomized first stage can estimate a local redeployment
fraction.  National model `rho`, national `a_alloc`, GDP growth, Gini and FGT2
remain outside this panel unless separate identification and transport evidence
is added.
