== Proposition

Let:

* stem:[F=23{,}090{,}075] be NTA FY2024 final-return persons;
* stem:[P=126{,}146{,}099] be the conservative candidate-person upper bound;
* stem:[M_L=122{,}494{,}621] be the conservative F71561 mother-person lower bound;
* stem:[D=8{,}085{,}259] be the deliberately adverse tracked-contamination allowance;
* stem:[C_{NR}\ge 0] be unresolved frame-incompatible final-return filers.

Define

[stem]
++++
B_0=\max\{0,F-(P-M_L)\}
++++

and the participation lower-bound frontier

[stem]
++++
L(C_{NR})=
\frac{\max\{0,B_0-D-C_{NR}\}}{P}.
++++

Then

[stem]
++++
B_0=19{,}438{,}597,
\qquad
B_0-D=11{,}353{,}338,
++++

so

[stem]
++++
L(0)=0.09000149897619902.
++++

For any prior admissibility threshold stem:[A], the prior is rejected whenever

[stem]
++++
C_{NR}<C^*_{NR}(A)
=
\max\{0,B_0-D-PA\}.
++++

For the recovered V6 prior stem:[A=0.022],

[stem]
++++
C^*_{NR}(0.022)=8{,}578{,}123.822.
++++

Therefore the `score_power=2.0` prior is rejected for every
stem:[C_{NR}<8{,}578{,}123.822].

== Proof

The F71561-compatible mother population contains at least stem:[M_L] persons
inside a universe containing at most stem:[P] candidate persons.  Hence at
most stem:[P-M_L] of the stem:[F] final-return persons can be placed outside
the mother population without invoking an additional frame mismatch.

The Fréchet lower overlap is therefore

[stem]
++++
B_0=\max\{0,F-(P-M_L)\}.
++++

Allocate the entire tracked allowance stem:[D] adversarially against this
overlap and then allocate unresolved frame-incompatible final-return filers
stem:[C_{NR}] in the same direction.  The remaining overlap cannot be smaller
than stem:[\max\{0,B_0-D-C_{NR}\}].

Dividing by the conservative candidate-person upper bound stem:[P] yields
stem:[L(C_{NR})].

For threshold stem:[A], rejection requires stem:[L(C_{NR})>A].  In the
positive-overlap region this is algebraically equivalent to

[stem]
++++
C_{NR}<B_0-D-PA.
++++

Truncating the critical value at zero gives the stated formula.  QED.
