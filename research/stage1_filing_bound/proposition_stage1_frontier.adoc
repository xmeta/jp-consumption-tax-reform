= Proposition: F71561 Stage-1 Robustness Frontier
:revdate: 2026-09-08
:stem: latexmath

== Objects

Let:

* stem:[F=23{,}090{,}075] be the observed NTA FY2024 Table 2-1 Final-return processing-row persons;
* stem:[P=126{,}146{,}099] be the 2020 Census population used as a deliberately loose person-denominator benchmark;
* stem:[M_0=122{,}494{,}621] be the published F71561 mother-population *frame benchmark* reconstructed from the 2020 Census-based survey design;
* stem:[D=8{,}085{,}259] be the deliberately adverse tracked mismatch allowance;
* stem:[C_U\ge0] be an analyst-specified upper budget for additional untracked reduction in the nominal overlap.

Importantly, stem:[M_0] is not asserted to be a lower bound on the actual set of
persons present in 2024.  The survey documentation states that the 2024 mother
population is estimated from the 2020 Census frame rearranged to 2024
municipality boundaries.

== Nominal bridge

Under exact set correspondence between the published frame benchmark and the
filing population, define the nominal Fréchet overlap benchmark

[stem]
++++
B_0=\max\{0,F-(P-M_0)\}=19{,}438{,}597.
++++

After allocating the full tracked allowance stem:[D] against that benchmark,

[stem]
++++
B_R=\max\{0,B_0-D\}=11{,}353{,}338.
++++

For an additional untracked mismatch budget stem:[C_U], define the robustness
frontier

[stem]
++++
R(C_U)=
\frac{\max\{0,B_R-C_U\}}{P}.
++++

This is a conditional robustness function, not an unconditional identified
filing-rate lower bound.

== Threshold proposition

For any externally supplied admissibility threshold stem:[A\in[0,1]], the
benchmark-adjusted participation rate remains above stem:[A] for every
untracked mismatch budget satisfying

[stem]
++++
C_U < C_U^*(A),
\qquad
C_U^*(A)=\max\{0,B_R-PA\}.
++++

Equivalently, the conclusion associated with threshold stem:[A] is robust to
any additional untracked overlap loss below stem:[C_U^*(A)].

== Proof

The first step is only a nominal benchmark.  If the published frame benchmark
stem:[M_0] were an exact subset cardinality inside the person universe
stem:[P], the Fréchet lower overlap with stem:[F] Table 2-1 Final-return processing-row persons would be

[stem]
++++
B_0=\max\{0,F-(P-M_0)\}.
++++

We then reduce that nominal overlap by the full tracked allowance stem:[D] and
by an additional untracked loss no larger than stem:[C_U].  Conditional on
that mismatch budget, the remaining overlap is no smaller than

[stem]
++++
\max\{0,B_0-D-C_U\}.
++++

Dividing by the deliberately loose person denominator stem:[P] gives
stem:[R(C_U)].  In the positive-overlap region,

[stem]
++++
R(C_U)>A
\iff
B_R-C_U>PA
\iff
C_U<B_R-PA.
++++

Truncating the critical mismatch budget at zero yields stem:[C_U^*(A)].
QED.

== Interpretation boundary

The proposition identifies the *break-even mismatch budget as a function of an
externally supplied threshold*.  It does not identify the actual value of
stem:[C_U], and therefore does not by itself reject any threshold
unconditionally.

A numerical application to recovered V6 thresholds belongs in the
recovery-only appendix until those threshold-generating source files are
restored and reproduced.
