= F71561 Stage-1 Filing Participation: Robustness Frontier
:revdate: 2026-09-08
:stem: latexmath

== Status

This module does not identify a point filing rate and does not promote
`income_tax` to READY.

The 2024 survey mother-population size is a published frame benchmark derived
from the 2020 Census design. It is not treated as a directly observed 2024
person-set lower bound.

== Reproduced benchmark

The official public counts used by the reproduced pipeline are:

* NTA FY2024 Table 2-1 Final-return processing-row persons: `23,090,075`;
* 2020 Census population used as a loose person denominator: `126,146,099`;
* F71561 mother-population frame benchmark: `122,494,621`;
* nominal exact-correspondence overlap benchmark: `19,438,597`.

Script 47 then allocates an intentionally adverse tracked mismatch allowance:

* all 2020--2024 births: `3,836,677`;
* all 2020--2024 gross international inflows: `2,643,204`;
* every 2024 death as one potential quasi-final return: `1,605,378`.

The allowance totals `8,085,259`, leaving a residual nominal benchmark of
`11,353,338` before any additional untracked mismatch.

== General robustness frontier

Let stem:[C_U\ge0] be an analyst-specified budget for additional untracked
reduction in nominal overlap. It includes any remaining timing, eligibility,
residence, or population-definition mismatch.

[stem]
++++
R(C_U)=
\frac{\max\{0,11{,}353{,}338-C_U\}}
{126{,}146{,}099}.
++++

For any externally supplied admissibility threshold stem:[A\in[0,1]], the
break-even total untracked mismatch budget is

[stem]
++++
C_U^*(A)=
\max\{0,11{,}353{,}338-126{,}146{,}099A\}.
++++

This function is the reproduced stage-1 result. It does not identify the actual
stem:[C_U] and therefore does not imply an unconditional threshold rejection.

Recovered V6 threshold values are kept outside the reproduced result set until
their source-generating chain is restored.

== Survey-frame facts

The 2024 National Survey of Family Income, Consumption and Wealth sample design
uses 2020 Census results rearranged to 2024 municipality boundaries. It
explicitly excludes Wajima, Suzu, Anamizu and Noto from the mother-population
estimate.

Table 4 reports `21,142,885` one-person general households, `602,771`
students, and `20,540,114` included single-person households.
