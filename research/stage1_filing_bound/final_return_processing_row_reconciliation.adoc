= NTA submitted-return vs Table 2-1 processing-row reconciliation
:toc:
:stem: latexmath

== Question

Does the FY2024 NTA Annual Statistics Table 2-1 row labelled +Final return+
represent the same statistical object as the NTA press-release count of income
tax final returns submitted by the following March 31?

No.

The publications use related but non-identical statistical scopes and reference
dates. The difference must be explicit before the stage-1 numerator is
interpreted.

== Official publication scopes

The NTA final-return-status press release Table 3-1 is displayed in thousand
persons. Its note states that the figures are based on returns submitted by
the following March 31.

For 2024 it displays:

[cols="2,1,1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
| Income category | Filing count, thousand | Positive tax | Refund | Zero
| Total | 23,389 | 5,175 | 13,533 | 4,681
| Business | 3,789 | 1,180 | 963 | 1,645
| Real estate | 1,497 | 806 | 174 | 516
| Salary | 11,442 | 2,389 | 7,711 | 1,343
| Miscellaneous | 5,825 | 417 | 4,292 | 1,116
| Other | 837 | 382 | 394 | 61
|===

The displayed five income categories do not always sum to the separately
displayed grand total because the publication is rounded at thousand-person
precision.

The annual Table 2-1 publication instead states that it reports taxation status
as of June 30, 2025 for persons who filed for 2024 or whose cases were processed
by correction/determination by March 31, 2025.

Therefore its +Final return+ row is a row in a filing-and-processing status
table observed at a later reference date.
== Exact Table 2-1 rows

The exact annual Table 2-1 counts are reproduced directly from the official
processing-status PDF.

[cols="2,1,1",options="header"]
|===
| Income category | +Final return+ processing row | Filed-or-processed total
| Total | 23,090,075 | 23,362,184
| Business | 3,730,986 | 3,785,184
| Real estate | 1,476,174 | 1,497,102
| Salary | 11,293,442 | 11,423,587
| Miscellaneous | 5,765,807 | 5,819,853
| Other | 823,666 | 836,458
|===

For the salary category the same Table 2-1 source further decomposes the
11,423,587 total into 11,293,442 on the +Final return+ row plus 130,145 in
amended-return, determination/correction, and request-for-correction
categories.

That decomposition is an exact processing-status identity. It does *not* by
itself prove that the press-publication difference is caused by those later
processing categories.

== Cross-publication comparison

For the total, the displayed press count center is:

[stem]
++++
23{,}389 \times 1000 = 23{,}389{,}000.
++++

Relative to Table 2-1 this is:

[stem]
++++
23{,}389{,}000 - 23{,}090{,}075 = 298{,}925
++++

above the +Final return+ processing row, and

[stem]
++++
23{,}389{,}000 - 23{,}362{,}184 = 26{,}816
++++

above the filed-or-processed total.

For salary the analogous displayed-center differences are 148,558 and 18,413
persons.

These are *not exact count differences*: the press values are displayed in
thousand persons. The generated artifact labels them as descriptive
display-center differences only.
== What is and is not identified

The official publications establish:

----
press Table 3-1
  = filing count based on returns submitted by March 31
  = displayed at thousand-person precision

annual Table 2-1
  = taxation status as of June 30
  = persons who filed or whose cases were processed by March 31
  = exact persons
----

They do not establish an exact crosswalk between the two publications.

In particular, this audit does not assume:

* that the press and Table 2-1 income-category definitions are exactly
  interchangeable;
* that every cross-publication difference is caused by amended returns;
* that the Table 2-1 +Final return+ row equals unique persons who submitted a
  return by the deadline.

Machine-readable statuses are:

----
press_and_table21_final_row_same_statistical_object = NO
press_vs_table21_category_definition_equivalence = NOT_ESTABLISHED
press_vs_table21_difference_mechanism = NOT_IDENTIFIED
----

== Stage-1 consequence

The stage-1 robustness frontier used the exact public value:

----
23,090,075
----

Changing this number is not required merely because the press publication uses
a different statistical object.

The correct resolution is semantic:

----
stage1_23090075_numerical_change_required = NO
stage1_23090075_wording_change_required   = YES
----

The canonical label is:

----
NTA Table 2-1 Final-return processing-row persons
----

The legacy data identifier +nta_final_return_filers_2024+ is retained for
backward compatibility and deterministic reproduction. It must not be read as
a claim that 23,090,075 is the count of all persons whose final-return forms
were submitted by March 31.

The stage-1 formula and all numerical frontier values remain unchanged.
