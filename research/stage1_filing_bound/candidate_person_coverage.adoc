= Candidate-person Denominator Lemma
:revdate: 2026-09-08
:stem: latexmath

== Purpose

The stage-1 denominator is intentionally not a fitted pseudo-filer count.
It is the entire 2020 Census population, `126,146,099` persons.

For the static Census frame, this is a deliberately loose upper benchmark for
the number of individual income-tax candidates.

== Lemma

Let stem:[T_0] be the number of candidate persons inside the static 2020 Census
person frame and stem:[P] the total Census population. Then

[stem]
++++
T_0 \le P=126{,}146{,}099.
++++

Replacing stem:[T_0] by stem:[P] can therefore only reduce the benchmark rate
conditional on a given overlap numerator.

== Scope

This lemma says nothing about exact correspondence between the 2020 person
frame and the 2024 survey/tax populations.

Children, institutional residents, and many other persons are deliberately
included in stem:[P]. Conversely, post-Census population changes, residence
status, survey eligibility, and other frame-definition differences are not
resolved by this denominator choice.

Those unresolved correspondence issues are represented by the total untracked
mismatch budget stem:[C_U] in the stage-1 robustness frontier.

== Consequence

The denominator itself is intentionally conservative within the static Census
frame. The substantive unresolved issue is the cross-time and cross-system
population correspondence, which remains explicit in stem:[C_U].
