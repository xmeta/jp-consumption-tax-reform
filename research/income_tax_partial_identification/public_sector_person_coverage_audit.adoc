= Public-sector person coverage audit
:toc:
:stem: latexmath

== Status

----
EXTERNAL_PUBLIC_WORKFORCE_BENCHMARK_NOT_NTA_DENOMINATOR
----

This audit asks whether current official public-employee statistics can fill
the person-count field missing from the NTA FY2024 source-withholding
+Public Offices+ salary table.

They cannot.

The National Personnel Authority and the Local Public Employee Salary Survey
do provide current person counts, but their statistical populations differ
from the NTA withholding population.  They are retained only as external
workforce benchmarks.

No v1-v5 endpoint or Paper 1 claim is changed.

== National public employee benchmark

The 2024 National Public Employee Salary Survey reports:

----
covered persons at 2024-04-01 = 250,434
----

The summary CSV and Table 1 independently reproduce the same total.

The official results document defines a narrower population than all persons
receiving salary from an NTA +Public Offices+ payer.  It covers permanent
employees subject to specified national pay statutes and excludes, among
others:

* new hires between January 16 and April 1;
* specified re-employed categories;
* employees at overseas establishments;
* employees on leave;
* certain dispatched employees;
* employees on childcare or other specified leave; and
* employees appointed for terms of one year or less.

It is therefore classified as:

----
EXTERNAL_NATIONAL_PUBLIC_WORKFORCE_BENCHMARK
----

and not as an NTA withholding denominator.
== Local public employee benchmark

The 2024 Local Public Employee Salary Survey Table 1 for all local public
bodies reports:

[cols="3,1",options="header"]
|===
| Published component | Persons
| total | *2,813,939*
| regular-category subtotal | 2,754,139
| general staff | 1,637,851
| education public employees | 856,644
| police | 259,644
| temporary staff | 59,800
|===

The published identities are exact:

[stem]
++++
2{,}754{,}139 + 59{,}800 = 2{,}813{,}939
++++

and

[stem]
++++
1{,}637{,}851 + 856{,}644 + 259{,}644 = 2{,}754{,}139.
++++

The all-body total also reproduces the published functional subtotals.

This source is classified as:

----
EXTERNAL_LOCAL_PUBLIC_WORKFORCE_BENCHMARK
----

It is not asserted identical to the NTA +Public Offices+ salary-recipient
population.
== Observable external subtotal

Pure arithmetic across the two external workforce statistics gives:

[stem]
++++
250{,}434 + 2{,}813{,}939 = 3{,}064{,}373.
++++

The repository retains this number only under the status:

----
EXTERNAL_PUBLIC_WORKFORCE_BENCHMARK_NOT_NTA_DENOMINATOR
----

The subtotal does not cover all national public-sector categories under a
common NTA tax-reporting definition, and the two input surveys have their own
coverage and timing rules.

It must not be relabeled as:

* NTA Public Offices salary recipients;
* public-sector taxpayers;
* final-return filers;
* persons with salary receipts above 20 million yen; or
* an additive correction to the Private Salary Survey.

== NTA Public Offices remains without a person denominator

The existing NTA source-withholding table directly reports:

[cols="3,1",options="header"]
|===
| Item | FY2024
| Public Offices salary/wages/bonus payments | 28,282,710 million yen
| Public Offices withholding tax | 917,029 million yen
| Public Offices salary-recipient persons | *NOT_IDENTIFIED*
|===

The current NTA table does not publish the corresponding salary-recipient
person count.

Consequently:

----
nta_public_offices_salary_person_count = NOT_IDENTIFIED
----

with status:

----
NTA_PUBLIC_OFFICES_PERSON_COUNT_NOT_IDENTIFIED
----
== Prohibited cross-frame calculations

A numerically possible but unidentified calculation would divide:

[stem]
++++
28{,}282{,}710	ext{ million yen}
++++

by the external subtotal:

[stem]
++++
3{,}064{,}373	ext{ persons}.
++++

The resulting quotient is not an identified NTA average salary because the
amount numerator and person denominator come from non-equivalent statistical
frames.

The machine-readable status is:

----
DO_NOT_DIVIDE_CROSS_FRAME_AMOUNT_BY_PERSON_SUBTOTAL
----

Likewise, the external workforce totals contain no compatible joint table of:

----
public/private employer class
x salary receipts >20m
x final-return status
x person
----

Therefore using these public-employee totals to adjust the Table 7
235,287-person >20m estimate is:

----
PROHIBITED
----

under status:

----
NO_PUBLIC_SECTOR_HIGH_SALARY_CROSSTAB
----

== Identification consequence

The evidence map is now:

----
NTA withholding statistics
  Public Offices
    payment amount       = observed
    withholding amount   = observed
    person count         = NOT_IDENTIFIED

external workforce statistics
  NPA covered national employees = 250,434
  local public employees          = 2,813,939
  arithmetic subtotal             = 3,064,373
       |
       | population definitions do not match NTA
       v
  NTA denominator substitution    = PROHIBITED
----

This strengthens the coverage audit but does not close the payroll-to-return
person-linkage gap.
