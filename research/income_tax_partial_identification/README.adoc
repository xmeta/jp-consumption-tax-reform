= Income-tax selection and partial-identification evidence
:revdate: 2026-09-08

== Scope

This directory separates three distinct objects:

. an external National Tax Agency primary-income-type positive-self-assessed-balance
  diagnostic;
. a cross-publication point check of primary-type composition;
. restricted-LP marginal-rate bounds, whose historical V6 implementation is
  still not reproduced.

Reproducing the first two objects does not identify the third.

== NTA primary-type stage-2 diagnostic

The official FY2024 NTA Annual Statistics Report, Table 2-2(1), reports exact
person counts for five primary income-earner categories.  Its denominator is
the Table 2-2(1) population: persons who filed for 2024 or whose income-tax
cases were processed by correction, determination, or related procedures under
the table's stated cutoff.  It is not identical to the separate stage-1
final-return benchmark population:

* business;
* real estate;
* salary;
* miscellaneous;
* other / not otherwise classified.

For category stem:[k], the reproduced diagnostic is

[stem]
++++
r_k = N^{+}_k / N^{T22}_k ,
++++

where stem:[N^{+}_k] is the exact number with positive self-assessed income tax
and stem:[N^{T22}_k] is the exact Table 2-2(1) denominator count in that NTA
category.

The resulting rates are stored in
`data/derived/nta_primary_type_stage2_2024.csv`.

These are observed NTA administrative category rates. They are *not* an
identified mapping from F71561 household deciles to filers. Applying them to
F71561 or to pseudo-filer units requires an additional transport/selection
assumption.

Scientific status:

----
EXTERNAL_STAGE2_DIAGNOSTIC_REPRODUCED
----

== Table 3-1 cross-publication point check

The NTA final-return press release reports Table 3-1 in thousand persons.
Its five displayed positive-self-assessed-balance category counts sum to 5,174 thousand,
while the separately displayed positive-self-assessed-balance grand total is 5,175
thousand. The table itself warns that totals and components can disagree due
to rounding.

For a category-composition comparison, the five displayed categories are
therefore normalized by their own displayed sum:

[stem]
++++
q^{T31}_k =
  N^{T31,+}_k /
  sum_j N^{T31,+}_j .
++++

The exact annual-statistics composition is

[stem]
++++
q^{annual}_k =
  N^{annual,+}_k /
  sum_j N^{annual,+}_j .
++++

The check reports

[stem]
++++
d_k = 100 (q^{T31}_k - q^{annual}_k)
++++

in percentage points.

The maximum absolute difference is

----
0.0844360351759 percentage point
----

which rounds to the historical V6 recovery target of

----
0.08444 percentage point.
----

The largest difference is for the miscellaneous-income category.

This exact recovery of the historical numerical target establishes that the
rounding/composition calculation has been reconstructed. It does not convert
the comparison into an independent holdout sample: both publications are NTA
administrative outputs concerning the same tax-year population.

Scientific status:

----
CROSS_PUBLICATION_POINT_CHECK_REPRODUCED
----

It is prohibited to describe this result as:

* confidence-interval coverage;
* independent-sample external validation;
* causal validation;
* validation of a filer-level MTR distribution;
* evidence that F71561 household-to-filer selection is identified.

== Sample Survey for Self-Assessment Income Tax

The official 2024 sample-survey report is retained as an additional raw source
because it documents the positive-self-assessed-tax target population and the
same five primary income categories.

It is not used to manufacture an independent validation claim. A calibration
or weighting design that anchors to administrative category totals would not
constitute an independent holdout merely because it appears in a different
publication.

== Pre-specified v2 rank bridge

A second model is now pre-specified in:

* `rank_bridge_lp_v2_spec.adoc`;
* `rank_bridge_lp_v2_config.csv`.

v2 uses the newly reproduced NTA Table 2-2(1) 25-income-class filing-status
cross-tab and maps only *rank decile to rank decile*.  It does not equate NTA
total net income to F71561 equivalized disposable income in yen.

Within any NTA income class that crosses a rank-decile boundary, the
positive-self-assessed-balance mass remains free subject to the published class total and
overlap capacity.  Thus v2 does not impose proportional-within-class
allocation.

The pre-specification was committed separately before any v2 endpoint was
computed.

v2 is now implemented in `run_rank_bridge_lp_v2.py` and tested by
`test_rank_bridge_lp_v2.py`.  Its generated artifacts are:

== Pre-specified v3 rank-transport budget

The next model is pre-specified in:

* `rank_bridge_lp_v3_spec.adoc`;
* `rank_bridge_lp_v3_config.csv`.

v3 replaces exact same-rank correspondence with a scaled doubly-stochastic
decile-rank coupling subject to a fixed average absolute rank-displacement
budget.  The budget grid is fixed before outcomes at
`0;0.25;0.5;1;2;3;4;5` decile-index units.  Positive self-assessed balance transport is
represented separately so that the program remains linear.

At displacement budget zero, the v3 feasible set must reproduce v2 exactly.
The public tables 7-141-1 and 7-171 remain interpretation-only diagnostics and
do not impose an NSFCW-to-NTA category crosswalk.

The pre-specification commit contains no v3 numerical result.

v3 is subsequently implemented in `run_rank_bridge_lp_v3.py` and verified by
`test_rank_bridge_lp_v3.py`.  Generated evidence is:

* `rank_bridge_lp_v3_minimum_relaxation_frontier.csv`;
* `rank_bridge_lp_v3_minimum_decile_diagnostics.csv`;
* `rank_bridge_lp_v3_zero_discrepancy_threshold.csv`;
* `rank_bridge_lp_v3_analytical_rate_floor.csv`;
* `rank_bridge_lp_v3_transport_plans.csv`;
* `rank_bridge_lp_v3_endpoints.csv`.

The analytical-rate-floor artifact separates the aggregate/non-negativity
compatibility floor from rank-transport restrictions.  Frontier MTR endpoints
at a minimum-discrepancy solution must not be interpreted as point
identification merely because their numerical interval can collapse: the
minimum-epsilon objective itself can force the pseudo-filer mixture to an
extreme scenario boundary.

v3 remains a model-contingent sensitivity and does not modify P1-C13 or
P1-C14.  Paper promotion remains separate and is prohibited until the
implementation commit passes CI.

== Pre-specified v4 fixed-budget sensitivity surface

v4 is pre-specified in:

* `rank_epsilon_surface_v4_spec.adoc`;
* `rank_epsilon_surface_v4_config.csv`.

It does not minimize the discrepancy budget.  Instead it evaluates the
Cartesian product of the already committed v3 rank-displacement grid and the
already committed v2 fixed-epsilon grid: 8 x 13 = 104 points.  Every point must
be reported in the feasibility surface, and all 84 v2/v3 endpoint objectives
must be evaluated at every feasible point.

This design separates conditional MTR-envelope sensitivity from the
minimum-epsilon objective that can force the pseudo-filer mixture to a scenario
boundary.  Delta and epsilon remain model budgets, not estimated error
parameters.

The pre-specification commit contains no v4 feasibility classifications,
endpoint values, or endpoint widths.

v4 is subsequently implemented in `run_rank_epsilon_surface_v4.py` and
verified by `test_rank_epsilon_surface_v4.py`.  Generated evidence is:

* `rank_epsilon_surface_v4_feasibility.csv`: all 104 fixed surface points;
* `rank_epsilon_surface_v4_endpoints.csv`: all 84 objectives at each feasible
  point;
* `rank_epsilon_surface_v4_summary.csv`: all 104 points with overall MTR
  minima, maxima, and widths when feasible.

The implementation checks both HiGHS-DS and HiGHS-IPM feasibility
classification at all 104 points, exact Delta=0 regression to the committed v2
fixed-epsilon artifacts, the committed v3 minimum-discrepancy boundary, and
two-dimensional feasible-set nesting as either budget is relaxed.

These outputs remain model-contingent sensitivity evidence.  A finite or narrow
conditional MTR envelope is not point identification.  Paper 1 promotion
remains prohibited until the implementation commit passes CI and a later,
separate claim-registry/manuscript commit is made.

== NTA self-assessed-balance semantic audit

A post-v4 terminology audit is recorded in
`self_assessed_balance_semantics_audit.adoc`.

The NTA count previously labelled `positive_liability` is the count of persons
with positive `申告納税額`: a positive self-assessed amount remaining after tax
credits and source withholding.  It is not the same event as the pseudo-filer
condition `income_tax > 0`, which is a modeled annual income-tax calculation.

The semantic schema migration is complete.  Active NTA fields now use
`positive_self_assessed_balance*`, the pseudo event is explicitly named
`pseudo_positive_modeled_annual_income_tax_share`, and the sample-survey
25x5 artifact uses the corresponding canonical path.

All 16 pre-migration numeric payload fingerprints in
`self_assessed_balance_numeric_anchors.csv` are reproduced exactly by
`test_self_assessed_balance_schema_migration.py`.  Thus v1-v4 numerical results
are unchanged.

`epsilon` is now explicitly interpreted as a cross-concept compatibility
budget rather than measurement error between two observations of the same
positive-tax event.

== NTA withholding / tax-credit bridge audit

`withholding_credit_bridge_audit.adoc` extends the semantic audit using the
2024 NTA Sample Survey Tables 1, 4 and 5.  The reproducible 125-cell artifact
`data/derived/nta_positive_self_assessed_balance_tax_flow_income_class_primary_type_2024.csv`
joins calculated tax, tax credits, source withholding and self-assessed balance
within the already positive-balance survey target.

The cell-level joins are exact across the three official workbooks.  The
published grand totals include 5,158,260 positive-balance persons, 3,302,359
persons with source withholding and 4,685,449 persons with at least one
reported tax-credit entry.  These imply within-target exposure rates of about
64.0% and 90.8%, respectively; neither is an all-calculated-tax-positive
population rate.

For a same-person same-year tax flow, positive self-assessed balance is a
downstream subset-type event relative to positive calculated tax.  This points
toward a one-sided compatibility bridge rather than the symmetric v1-v4 gap.
However, the current pseudo calculation is a modeled 2026 ordinary income-tax
object built from 2024 household aggregates, so the cross-system inequality is
not treated as an observed identity.  A v5 model must be pre-specified in a
separate commit before any v5 result is computed.

Audit-stage status before pre-specification: `V5_RESULT_NOT_COMPUTED`.

The subsequent v5 design was pre-specified in
`rank_one_sided_bridge_v5_spec.adoc` and
`rank_one_sided_bridge_v5_config.csv`.  It replaces the symmetric event-rate
gap with a one-sided directional-violation budget `eta`, preserves the v3 rank
transport grid, and fixes solver/tolerance/nesting checks before any v5 result
is available.  The original `fef4776` version of
`test_rank_one_sided_bridge_v5_prespec.py` enforced that the pre-specification
commit contained no v5 implementation, generated result, or Paper 1 P1-C17
claim.  The implementation commit `006e259` also kept Paper 1 at 16 claims and
passed GitHub CI before promotion.  Git history preserves both gates; the
current test SHA-locks the exact spec/config and verifies the later separate
P1-C17 promotion without changing the frozen v5 specification.

The pre-specification state at commit `fef4776` is
`PRESPECIFIED_NO_V5_RESULT`; the exact spec/config bytes remain SHA-256 locked
by `test_rank_one_sided_bridge_v5_prespec.py`.

The later implementation in `run_rank_one_sided_bridge_v5.py` follows that
frozen specification.  The first result is:

* `eta_star = 0` at every pre-specified Delta in
  `0, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5`;
* zero directional violation is feasible already at `Delta = 0`;
* both HiGHS-DS and HiGHS-IPM reproduce those frontier/threshold results;
* an independent reduced LP, separate from the v5 inequality builder, also
  verifies Delta=0 / eta=0 feasibility;
* exactly 672 pre-specified endpoint rows are produced;
* the equal-decile overall taxable-income-weighted MTR envelope is
  10.0884%--12.8680%, and the liability-weighted envelope is
  10.8806%--13.7776%, at each pre-specified Delta.

This means the positive v2/v3 symmetric discrepancy is not required as a
*directional subset violation* once the tax events are ordered correctly.  It
does not establish person-level linkage, true rank correspondence, a causal
bound, or a statistically sharp identified set.  The v5 endpoint set is also
not claimed to nest the v3 endpoint set because the two are evaluated at
different optimized compatibility budgets.

Current implementation status:
`MODEL_CONTINGENT_RANK_ONE_SIDED_SUBSET_COMPATIBILITY`.
The implementation commit itself left Paper 1 at 16 claims.  After its push,
PR, and main post-merge CI succeeded, the result was promoted separately as
P1-C17 with status
`MODEL_CONTINGENT_RANK_ONE_SIDED_BRIDGE_REPRODUCED`.

== Calculated-tax-positive denominator audit

`calculated_tax_positive_denominator_audit.adoc` checks whether the official
NTA long-term Sample Survey workbooks supply the missing person denominator for
positive calculated tax.  They do not.  The 2024 long-term Table 1 taxpayer
count and the Table 2 `納税者数（合計）` total are both 5,158,260, exactly the
independently reproduced positive-self-assessed-balance survey target.  The
same long-term workbooks separately report 8,044,571 million yen of calculated
tax, but no person count for calculated tax > 0 outside the already
positive-balance-conditioned target.

Within the FY2024 Table 2-2(1) filed/processed population, the same-person
same-year event ordering yields only the broad bound
`5,158,260 <= N(calculated tax > 0) <= 23,362,184`, or
22.0795%--100%.  The upper end is explicitly scoped to that administrative
population; it is not a general-population, F71561, or withholding-only
settlement upper bound.  Consequently the long-term `納税者数` series must not
be used to calibrate the pseudo calculated-tax-positive rate or `eta`.

Current audit status:
`CALCULATED_TAX_POSITIVE_PERSON_COUNT_NOT_IDENTIFIED`.

== Private-salary taxpayer-status external diagnostic

`private_salary_taxpayer_status_diagnostic.adoc` adds the 2024 NTA Private
Salary Survey Table 16 as an external wage-side scale diagnostic.  The official
methodology includes private-sector salary earners regardless of tax status and
defines `納税者` as salary earners with positive source-withheld income tax.
The four Table 16 panels are reproduced across all fourteen salary classes,
including the versions that exclude secondary-payroll (`乙欄`) records.

After `乙欄` exclusion, the year-end-adjusted panels contain 35,556,416
taxpayers among 52,797,680 salary earners, or 67.3447%, with 7,280,879 million
yen of tax.  This is materially informative about the scale of positive payroll
income tax outside the positive-self-assessed-balance sample.  It is not,
however, asserted to be a strict lower bound for final calculated-tax-positive
persons: a later final return can change tax through deductions, credits, or
loss offsets.

The payroll count is also not added to the 5,158,260 positive-self-assessed-
balance persons because the public sources do not identify person overlap.
The invalid naive sum 40,714,676 is explicitly guarded against by the test.
The diagnostic is therefore not used to calibrate the pseudo calculated-tax-
positive rate, v5 `eta`, or a cross-source union count.

Current payroll-diagnostic status:
`EXTERNAL_PAYROLL_TAX_DIAGNOSTIC_NO_CROSS_SOURCE_UNION`.

== Private-salary to final-return overlap audit

`salary_return_overlap_audit.adoc` extends the wage-side diagnostic with
Private Salary Survey Table 19 and the full Table 2-2(1) salary-primary
final-return population.

Table 19 exactly decomposes the published no-year-end-adjustment payroll
population by secondary-payroll (`乙欄`), previous-employer-salary-unknown,
and other reasons, subject only to the documented publication-rounding
residuals.  For full-year taxpayers it reports 3,729,740 no-year-end-adjustment
records: 1,700,196 secondary-payroll, zero previous-employer-unknown, and
2,029,544 other-reason records.

Its high-salary panel reports 320,983 full-year salary earners with salary
receipts above 20 million yen.  The 2024 NTA filing guidance makes that a
statutory filing-relevant group, but the public aggregate tables do not observe
the corresponding person-level final-return match.

On the return side, the 25 salary-primary Table 2-2(1) classes sum to
11,423,587 cases: 2,385,726 positive self-assessed balance, 7,697,018 refund,
and 1,340,843 residual.  Within the positive-balance salary-primary target,
2,161,129 have some source withholding and 2,114,353 have salary-source
withholding.

The audit therefore sharpens the administrative transition map without
identifying the cross-source person overlap.  Neither
35,556,416 + 5,158,260 nor 35,556,416 + 11,423,587 is a valid union.

Current overlap-audit status:
`OFFICIAL_OVERLAP_AUDIT_EXACT_PERSON_MATCH_NOT_IDENTIFIED`.
