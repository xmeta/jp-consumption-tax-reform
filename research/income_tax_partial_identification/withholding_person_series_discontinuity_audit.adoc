= NTA withholding salary person-series discontinuity audit
:toc:
:stem: latexmath

== Status

----
PUBLISHED_PERSON_SERIES_DISCONTINUED_AFTER_2006
----

This audit determines whether historical NTA withholding statistics can recover
the current Public Offices/Others salary-recipient person denominator.

They cannot.

The long-term NTA withholding workbook preserves a person series for salary
payments, but the person fields are published only through 2006.  From 2007
through 2024 the person cells are shown as unavailable while payment amounts
continue.

No v1-v5 endpoint or Paper 1 claim is changed.

== Official long-term note

For the salary-income long-term table, the NTA note states that the person
fields from 1991 onward are estimates based on a sample survey.

Accordingly this audit treats 1991-2006 person values as:

----
SAMPLE_SURVEY_ESTIMATE_PUBLISHED
----

not as exact administrative counts.

The audit deliberately begins in 1991, where the official note provides an
unambiguous common classification for the person fields.
== 2006: last published person estimate

The 2006 Table (7) publishes Public Offices, Others and Total columns with
person estimates, payment amounts and withholding amounts.

For salary, wages and bonus:

[cols="3,1,1",options="header"]
|===
| Classification | Persons, thousand | Payment, million yen
| Public Offices | *9,170* | 31,894,770
| Others | *66,936* | 210,904,546
| Public + Others | *76,106* | 242,799,316
|===

The person identity is exact at the published thousand-person precision:

[stem]
++++
9{,}170 + 66{,}936 = 76{,}106.
++++

The 2006 table note also states that the person count includes short-term and
temporary employment, including part-time workers.

The long-term-series note classifies the 2006 person fields as sample-survey
estimates.  Therefore 76,106 thousand is not treated as an exact administrative
count or as a verified annual-unique-person count.
== 2007: person columns disappear

The 2007 Table (7) continues to publish salary payment and withholding amounts
for Public Offices, Others and Total, but the person columns no longer appear.

The long-term workbook confirms the structural break:

----
1991-2006  person fields = published sample-survey estimates
2007-2024  person fields = not published
----

This produces:

----
person_series_last_published_year = 2006
person_series_first_missing_year  = 2007
missing years through 2024        = 18
----

Machine-readable status:

----
PUBLISHED_PERSON_SERIES_DISCONTINUED
----

The payment-amount series itself remains continuous, so this is specifically a
loss of the person dimension rather than a disappearance of the salary-source
statistics.

== Historical ratio is not a current estimator

The 2006 historical Public Offices person share is:

[stem]
++++
9{,}170 / 76{,}106
=
0.120489843114.
++++

This is only a historical ratio of published sample-survey estimates.

It must not be used as a 2024 Public Offices person share.
== Amount composition changed materially

The Public Offices share of salary/wages/bonus payment amount was:

[stem]
++++
31{,}894{,}770 /
(31{,}894{,}770 + 210{,}904{,}546)
=
0.131362684728
++++

in 2006.

For 2024 the corresponding amount share is:

[stem]
++++
28{,}282{,}710 /
(28{,}282{,}710 + 325{,}610{,}351)
=
0.079918803494.
++++

The change is:

[stem]
++++
-5.144388123434
++++

percentage points.

Thus even the observable payment composition changed substantially between the
last person-estimate year and the current year.

This does not prove how the person share changed, but it is direct evidence
against silently carrying the 2006 person composition forward unchanged.

== Identification consequence

The prohibited operation is:

----
2024 Public Offices persons
  := 2024 salary persons x 2006 Public Offices person share
----

Its status is:

----
DO_NOT_EXTRAPOLATE_DISCONTINUED_SAMPLE_PERSON_SERIES
----

The current result remains:

----
nta_public_offices_person_count_2024 = NOT_IDENTIFIED
----

Historical person estimates do not close the current payroll-to-return bridge.
== Relation to the external public-workforce audit

The separate public-sector person coverage audit supplies current external
benchmarks:

----
National Personnel Authority covered employees = 250,434
Local Public Employee Salary Survey total       = 2,813,939
External arithmetic subtotal                    = 3,064,373
----

Those counts cannot substitute for the NTA Public Offices denominator because
their population definitions differ.

The present audit independently shows that the NTA's own salary-source person
series cannot supply a current denominator either, because the published series
stops after 2006.

The two audits therefore converge on the same identification conclusion through
different evidence paths.
