:toc:

== Status

CALCULATED_TAX_POSITIVE_PERSON_COUNT_NOT_IDENTIFIED

This audit asks whether the National Tax Agency's public long-term Sample
Survey for Self-Assessment Income Tax supplies the missing person denominator
for positive *calculated* income tax.

It does not alter v1-v5 model results or Paper 1 claims.

== Official sources retained

The repository now retains the official NTA long-term workbooks:

* `NTA-SHINKOKU-JIKEIRETSU-T1-XLSX`:
  long-term Table 1, Summary;
* `NTA-SHINKOKU-JIKEIRETSU-T2-XLSX`:
  long-term Table 2, income-class series.

The existing official sources

* `NTA-2024-SHINKOKU-SAMPLE`; and
* `NTA-R06`

provide the 2024 survey-target and annual-statistics definitions used for the
semantic cross-check.

* income amount;
* deductions;
* taxable income;

== Why the taxpayer count is not the missing denominator

The NTA defines the Sample Survey target as persons who have positive
self-assessed income tax. Persons with income but no positive self-assessed
balance, including refund filers, are outside that survey target.

The independently reproduced FY2024 annual-statistics total for persons with
positive self-assessed balance is also exactly:

[stem]
++++
N_{B>0}=5{,}158{,}260.
++++

This exactly matches both long-term taxpayer-count totals above.

Therefore the long-term `納税者数` series is the positive-self-assessed-balance
survey population. It cannot be reinterpreted as

[stem]
++++
N_{T^{calc}>0}.
++++

== Directional implication and the only public-data bound currently justified

For one person in one tax year,

[stem]
++++
B>0
Longrightarrow
T^{calc}>0.
++++

Therefore, within the FY2024 Table 2-2(1) filed/processed population,

[stem]
++++
N_{T^{calc}>0}
ge
5{,}158{,}260.
++++

The same annual-statistics population contains 23,362,184 persons in total.
Without person-level or sufficiently detailed cross-tabs linking calculated tax
to the final balance, a conservative within-population upper bound is only

[stem]
++++
N_{T^{calc}>0}
le
23{,}362{,}184.
++++

Hence

[stem]
++++
5{,}158{,}260
le
N_{T^{calc}>0}
le
23{,}362{,}184,
++++

or, as a share of that Table 2-2(1) population,

[stem]
++++
0.220795281811
le
Pr(T^{calc}>0)
le
1.
++++

The width is 18,203,924 persons. This is too broad to replace the current
model-contingent bridge.

The upper bound is *only* a Table 2-2(1)-population bound. It is not an upper
bound for the Japanese population, F71561 household members, all wage earners,
or all persons with positive calculated income tax under withholding-only
settlement.

== What remains unobserved

Public sources examined here do not provide a person count for calculated-tax
positive persons outside the positive-self-assessed-balance Sample Survey
target.

In particular, the long-term series does not identify

[stem]
++++
Pr(B>0mid T^{calc}>0),
++++

because its person denominator is already conditioned on stem:[B>0].

The missing high-value evidence remains a directly comparable tax-unit table
or microdata source that observes, for the same population and year:

. calculated-tax-positive status;
. tax-credit status/amount;
. source-withholding status/amount;
. final self-assessed balance status; and
. income rank or a sufficiently detailed income class.

== Consequence for v5

This audit supports the v5 *direction* but does not identify its cross-system
transition probability.

The long-term taxpayer count must not be used to calibrate the pseudo modeled
calculated-tax-positive rate or a numerical stem:[eta] value.

P1-C17 remains a model-contingent directional compatibility result. The audit
does not turn it into observed person-level linkage or point identification.
