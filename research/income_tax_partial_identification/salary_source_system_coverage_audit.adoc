= Salary source-system coverage audit
:toc:
:stem: latexmath

== Status

----
MONETARY_COVERAGE_DIAGNOSTIC_NO_PERSON_INFERENCE
----

This audit compares two official 2024 NTA salary-side statistical systems:

* the FY2024 withholding-income-tax administrative table for employment income;
* the 2024 Private Salary Survey.

Its purpose is not to merge the two systems.  It quantifies their monetary
coverage differences and fixes the resulting identification boundary for the
private-salary to final-return bridge.

No v1-v5 endpoint or Paper 1 claim is changed.

== Source systems

=== Withholding-income-tax statistics

The FY2024 withholding-income-tax Table (7) reports salary, wages and bonus
payment amounts and withholding-tax amounts under:

* +Public Offices+;
* +Others+; and
* +Total+.

The same table reports wages of day laborers as a separate row.

The official note states that +Public Offices+ includes government
organizations, local authorities and specified related organizations.  The
table is compiled from information-return total tables and withholding
collection statements.

No salary-recipient person count is published in this table.

=== Private Salary Survey

The official methodology states that the Private Salary Survey target is
salary earners working, as of December 31, for withholding agents that are
private establishments.

The survey uses two-stage sampling of establishments and salary earners.  For
sample establishments, salary earners with annual salary above 20 million yen
are all selected at the second sampling stage.

Therefore the Private Salary Survey and the withholding administrative table
are not interchangeable statistical frames.
== Administrative amount totals

For salary, wages and bonus, the withholding-income-tax table reports:

[cols="3,1,1",options="header"]
|===
| Classification | Payment, million yen | Withholding tax, million yen
| Public Offices | 28,282,710 | 917,029
| Others | 325,610,351 | 11,021,742
| Total | *353,893,061* | *11,938,771*
|===

The identities

[stem]
++++
28{,}282{,}710 + 325{,}610{,}351 = 353{,}893{,}061
++++

and

[stem]
++++
917{,}029 + 11{,}021{,}742 = 11{,}938{,}771
++++

are exact in the published table.

Day-labor wages are separately reported at:

* 1,232,032 million yen of payments;
* 20,909 million yen of withholding tax.

The displayed Public Offices plus Others day-labor payment components differ
from their printed total by one million yen because of publication rounding;
the executable audit permits only that observed amount residual.

== Private Salary Survey Table 1

Private Salary Survey Table 1 reports for 2024:

[cols="3,1",options="header"]
|===
| Item | Published estimate
| average-monthly salary earners | 60,184,788 persons
| salary amount | 241,438,813 million yen
| tax amount | 11,183,370 million yen
|===

The 60,184,788 figure is an average-monthly survey estimate.  It is not
classified as an annual unique-person count and is not added to or directly
compared as a person union with filed-or-processed income-tax populations.
== Public-office amount shares

Within the withholding administrative salary/wages/bonus amounts:

[stem]
++++
s^{pay}_{public}
=
28{,}282{,}710 / 353{,}893{,}061
=
0.079918803494
++++

and

[stem]
++++
s^{tax}_{public}
=
917{,}029 / 11{,}938{,}771
=
0.076811005086.
++++

These are *amount shares*, not person shares.

The source table publishes no salary-recipient count by Public Offices versus
Others.  Consequently:

----
public_offices_salary_person_share = NOT_IDENTIFIED
----

The 7.99% payment share must not be applied to the Table 7 positive-balance
salary-receipt person estimate.

In particular, the operation

----
235,287 x (1 - public-office payment share)
----

is prohibited as a private-sector person-count adjustment.

Machine-readable status:

----
DO_NOT_APPLY_AMOUNT_SHARE_TO_PERSON_COUNT
----
== Why Others is not the Private Salary Survey universe

A tempting interpretation would be to treat +Others+ in the withholding table
as the same universe as the Private Salary Survey.  The published aggregates
do not support that interpretation.

The salary-amount comparison is:

[stem]
++++
241{,}438{,}813 / 325{,}610{,}351
=
0.741496123383.
++++

This is only a descriptive cross-statistic amount ratio.  It is not a 74.15%
coverage probability.

More decisively, the tax-amount comparison is:

[stem]
++++
11{,}183{,}370 / 11{,}021{,}742
=
1.014664469555.
++++

The Private Salary Survey published tax estimate is larger than the
withholding table's +Others+ tax aggregate.

Equivalently:

[stem]
++++
11{,}021{,}742 - 11{,}183{,}370
=
-161{,}628
++++

million yen.

If the two published aggregates were a simple nested-subset pair under a
common measurement system, this sign would be impossible.

The audit therefore classifies the ratio as:

----
CROSS_STATISTIC_RATIO_NOT_SUBSET_RATE
----

This is evidence of statistical-frame / timing / aggregation non-equivalence,
not evidence that either official table is erroneous.
== Payment differences are not missing-person residuals

The published payment differences are:

[stem]
++++
325{,}610{,}351 - 241{,}438{,}813
=
84{,}171{,}538
++++

million yen between source-system +Others+ and the Private Salary Survey, and

[stem]
++++
353{,}893{,}061 - 241{,}438{,}813
=
112{,}454{,}248
++++

million yen between the source-system total and the Private Salary Survey.

Neither amount is classified as:

* missing private payroll;
* missing private salary earners;
* a public-sector person count;
* a payroll/final-return overlap residual; or
* a denominator for a filing probability.

Their status is:

----
DIFFERENCE_NOT_MISSING_PRIVATE_PAYROLL
----

The source systems differ in frame and aggregation design, so amount
differences cannot be inverted into person counts without additional
identifying information.
== Consequence for the >20m salary-receipt bridge

The previous bridge established:

* Private Salary Survey Table 19: 320,983 full-year private-sector salary
  earners with salary receipts above 20 million yen;
* Sample Survey Table 7: 235,287 positive-self-assessed-balance taxpayers with
  salary receipts above 20 million yen.

The new withholding-income-tax source confirms that public-office salary
payments form a nonzero part of the broader salary-source system.  However, it
does not publish the corresponding number or high-salary distribution of
public-office salary recipients.

Therefore the new source strengthens the reason why the two >20m counts cannot
be directly interpreted as an overlap rate, while it does not provide the
missing person-level correction.

The remaining missing quantity is still a person-level or sufficiently
cross-tabulated bridge such as:

----
private/public employer class
x salary-receipt threshold
x final-return status
x person
----

No such joint public table has been identified in the sources registered by
this audit.
