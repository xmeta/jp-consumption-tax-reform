= NTA Theme 1 joint-research feasibility audit
:revdate: 2026-09-12

== Question

Can the National Tax Agency / National Tax College restricted Theme 1 return
microdata materially shrink the salary-to-final-return identification gap?

== Official data contract

The sixth-call Theme 1 schema covers 2014--2024 individual income-tax returns.
The checked official sample is synthetic, not real taxpayer data.

`data/derived/nta_joint_research_theme1_field_presence_2014_2024.csv` shows
that the advertised schema contains, for every year 2014--2024:

* a dedicated joint-research identifier and tax year;
* filing date and filing type;
* salary receipts and salary income;
* source-withholding tax and self-assessed balance;
* third-installment payment and refund fields; and
* core deduction fields.

Salary-receipt class is present from 2020 and the explicit amended-return tax
increase field from 2022.  These differences are preserved rather than
back-filled.

== Identification gain

Theme 1 materially improves the *return-side* evidence set.  Within an approved
individual-return file, the advertised fields permit direct observation of
salary receipts, source withholding, deductions, filing metadata, self-assessed
balance, and payment/refund outcomes.  The dedicated joint-research identifier
and repeated tax years make longitudinal return-side reconciliation feasible.

This is narrower than the missing public-data bridge.  Theme 1 does not advertise
a Private Salary Survey person identifier, does not observe non-filers, and does
not expose private-payroll year-end-adjustment status.  Therefore:

----
return-side longitudinal tax-flow bridge = MATERIAL_PARTIAL_IDENTIFICATION_GAIN
private-payroll-to-return person link     = NOT_IDENTIFIED
----

No salary amount, rank, demographic field, or model-based matching rule may be
used as a substitute for an approved cross-source person identifier.

== Access and operational constraints

The 2026-03-01 guideline and current FAQ require individual-data academic
applicants to be full-time researchers at an eligible public, independent,
university, or inter-university institution.  A specific person's eligibility
must be checked at application time; private-business affiliation alone is not
sufficient.
Approved individual data are used only on NTA-provided terminals at the National
Tax College Wako campus.  The FAQ gives the ordinary access window as non-holiday
weekdays 09:30--17:00 with advance reservation.  Individual-data users are
appointed visiting professors and are subject to the associated confidentiality
obligations.  Raw microdata and unreviewed intermediate outputs cannot be copied
out of the secure environment.

The guideline normally caps the approved use period at three years.  Research
outputs must be reported to NTA before publication and pass the prescribed
consistency, confidentiality, and disclosure checks.  This administrative
review is not scientific validation and must not promote a repository claim by
itself.

The FAQ permits a proposal to combine eligible statistical microdata with the
tax records for non-identifying statistical research.  Such linkage is not
pre-approved: the external-data application, exact keys, processing time, and
any external-data fee remain application-specific.  Any such fee is borne by
the user.  No project-wide monetary cost is inferred from the reviewed sources.

== Decision for Issue 25

The expected identification gain is material enough to justify a future-call
proposal, but only for the return-side longitudinal question.  The proposal is
recorded in `nta_joint_research_theme1_proposal.adoc`.

The proposal deliberately does not claim to solve the private-payroll overlap.
Adding external statistical microdata is an optional later extension only if an
eligible source, lawful join mechanism, and provider approvals are established.

This audit does not change `data/scientific_state.csv`.  Until restricted data
are actually obtained and analyzed, the existing public-data scientific states
remain authoritative.
